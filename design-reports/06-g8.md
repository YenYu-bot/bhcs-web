# g8-drills.html (mulformula / polyops / factor) design review

Source: `/home/claude/yenyu-bot/bhcs-web/tools/math/g8-drills.html` (branch `fix/math-answer-errors`), `mulformulaConfig` L932–1063, `polyopsConfig` L1065–1196, `factorConfig` L1198–1255, engine `generate()` L1691.

## Summary

1. **Engine-level (all three topics, P1):** `mkUnit`/`unit` read `mode=ctx.modes[0]` (L954, L1092, L1239; also L1282/1417/1526 in other topics). With the default UI (整數+分數+小數 all ticked) every question is integer; 分數/小數 only appear when 整數 is unticked. geoseq (L367) does `pick(ctx.modes)` — copy that.
2. **Difficulty ladder is a sliding window, not a ladder.** mulformula/polyops pick kinds `[0,1,2] / [1,2,3] / [2,3,4]`, so basic and advanced share 2 of 3 forms, advanced and challenge share 2 of 3, and several units have the *easiest* scaffolded form at index 2 (appears at all three levels, e.g. 挑戰 gets 「利用 (10＋6)² 計算 16²」). Factor uses `levelMax` 5/8/12, so for most factor units 挑戰 = bigger numbers only.
3. **分數/小數 modes produce arithmetic no Taiwanese worksheet would print** wherever two or more non-integer coefficients get multiplied: formulaChain `x⁴ − 231.3441`, nested `0.294x² − 0.55x ＋ 0.042`, binomialMultiply `(50557)/(800)x`, longDivision `(−(63)/(8)x³ − (443)/(24)x² …) ÷ ((7)/(2)x ＋ (5)/(2))`. Factor's fraction/decimal mode is only ever 「整數題 × 一個分數常數 k」 (`scaleFor`), never `x² − 1/4`, `x² ＋ x ＋ 1/4`, `0.25x² − 1`.
4. **Two dead/buggy kinds (P1):** `combine` never shuffles its terms (`shuffle(ex)` return value discarded, L1096) so 同類項 are always adjacent; `conjugateExpand` kind 2 renders identically to kind 1 because `norm` re-sorts the factors, so the promised 「交換順序」 form never appears.
5. **Answers not in teacher form (P1):** repeated factors printed as `(5x − 1)(5x − 1)` instead of `(5x − 1)²` (nonmonicTrinomial, grouping); grouping can emit `− 84x ＋ 84x` that cancels; 差平方速算 produces 「利用 (10−9)² 計算 1²」.
6. **Missing core 國二 types:** 完全平方式求 k, 二元齊次十字交乘 `x²＋5xy＋6y²`, `4x²−9y²`, 共同括號變號 `x(x−2)−3(2−x)`, 反求/填空 for 乘法公式 `(x＋□)²＝x²＋8x＋□`, 常數在前 `(5＋x)(5−x)`, 降冪排列/係數判讀 for 多項式.
7. **Beyond 108課綱 國二:** `splitTerms` (quartic `(x²＋bx＋c)(x²＋d)`), `multivariable` 二元雙十字, `completing` x⁴ 型, and long division by a 二次式 are 高一/資優 material. Keep them only in 挑戰 and label them; replace splitTerms' content with 國中 拆項/三一分組.
8. Wording and tips are generally clean and textbook-like; ordering of answers (降冪, x before y) is consistent. Integer mode at the preset levels is usable today; the work is in ladders, modes and missing types.

## Topic: mulformula — 乘法公式
- Levels promised: 基礎「整十整百為中心的速算、直接套三個公式展開」／進階「大數速算、明寫公式、交換順序與負號在前、三項式平方、a＋b 與 ab 求 a²＋b²」／挑戰「速算後再加減、含 x²、公式相減、連鎖、由和差求 ab」.
- Overall: 12 units × 5 kinds, structurally rich, but the `[0,1,2]/[1,2,3]/[2,3,4]` window makes 2/3 of each level identical to its neighbour; the 「交換順序」 promise is unfulfilled (dead kind); fraction/decimal expansion coefficients are too ugly for print; no 填空/反求 or 應用 forms at all.

### Unit nearProduct 中心數乘積
- Variety: 5 kinds (plain, 「計算…的值」 with reversed order, 「先寫成 (c＋d)(c−d)」 scaffold, `u×w − c²`, `(c＋d)(c−d)(c²＋d²)` chain). 3 per level, good. Kind 4 ignores mode (fraction/decimal 挑戰 show integer `11 × 9 × (10² ＋ 1²)`).
- Difficulty: basic already includes the "large" kind (`計算 39 × 61`, `487 × 513` up to 1000-centre), while 挑戰 keeps the scaffold kind `用平方差公式計算 26 × 14，先寫成…`. Ladder is flat.
- Plausibility/tip/mode: tip good. Fraction mode (`20½ × 21½`) matches textbooks, but answer `(1763)/(4)` — teachers expect `440 3/4` since the whole method is `21² − ¼`. Decimal fine.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Re-window: basic = kinds {scaffold, plain small}, advanced = {plain small, large-reversed, `u×w − c²`}, challenge = {large, `u×w − c²`, chain}. Concretely use per-level kind lists instead of the global window.
  2. [P2] 速算 units (nearProduct, differenceValue, sumNumber, differenceNumber, comprehensive): in fraction mode print the answer as 帶分數 (`sh(v,mode,true)`), e.g. `Q: 10½ × 9½＝  A: 99 3/4`.
  3. [P3] Kind 4 chain: honour mode (fraction `(10＋½)(10−½)(100＋¼)`), or restrict kind 4 to integer and substitute `u×w − c²` in other modes.

### Unit differenceValue 平方差求值
- Variety: 5 kinds (`u²−w²`, 相差 1 hint, reversed negative, `÷(u＋w)`, `u²−w²−e²`). Good coverage of 參考書 forms.
- Difficulty: basic has `求 487² − 513² 的值` (negative, 3-digit) and `866² − 865²`; fine with formula but basic 「相差 1」 hint text `（兩數相差 1）` is a giveaway that also appears in advanced. 挑戰 OK.
- Plausibility/tip/mode: good. Fraction `((14 2/5)² − (13 3/5)²) ÷ 28 ＝ 4/5` is a nice form.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P3] Drop the parenthetical `（兩數相差 1）` from the advanced copy of kind 1; keep it in basic only.
  2. [P2] Add 應用 kind for 挑戰: 「2024² − 2023² ＝」 and 「利用公式計算 999² − 1」 (年份型 會考常見).

### Unit sumNumber 和平方速算
- Variety: 5 kinds (`n²＝`, 「計算 1005²」, 「利用 (40＋4)² 計算 44²」, `n² − c²`, `u² − w²（分別展開）`).
- Difficulty: **inverted**. Kind 2 (the scaffold 「利用 (10＋6)² 計算 16²」) is at index 2 so it appears at every level including 挑戰; 挑戰 also gets `60² − 50²`. Kind 1 (1005², 1009²) sits in basic.
- Plausibility/tip/mode: `cen` lets d be up to 9 with c＝10 → 「利用 (10＋9)² 計算 19²」 (19 should be 20−1). Fraction `(5＋3/5)²` and decimal `(7＋0.6)²` are textbook-like.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Reorder kinds to {scaffold, small plain, large plain, `n²−c²`, 分別展開} and use per-level lists basic {0,1}, advanced {1,2,3}, challenge {3,4 + new}. Also cap `d ≤ c/2` in `cen` for 和差平方 so 19² is never built from (10＋9)².
  2. [P2] New 挑戰 kind: 「已知 a＋b＝7、ab＝12，求 (a＋b)² − 4ab」 is covered elsewhere; instead add 「計算 (101² − 99²) ÷ 2」 or 「1001² − 999²」 style.

### Unit differenceNumber 差平方速算
- Variety: 5 kinds; kind 3 `c² − n²` (`50² − 46²`) is really a 平方差, not a 差平方 drill; kind 4 `u² ＋ w²` (`55² ＋ 45²`) is good.
- Difficulty: same inversion as sumNumber; `利用 (10−9)² 計算 1²` and `利用 (3−½)² 計算 (2½)²` are nonsense (「`Q: 利用 (10−9)^2 計算 1^2 A: 1`」).
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Require `d ≤ c/4` (integer) / `d ≤ 1` (fraction, decimal) for the scaffold kind, so n is always 「整十減一點點」 (17, 27, 46, 983, 8¾, 4.8).
  2. [P2] Replace kind 3 with 「`(100 − 3)² − 100²`」 style? No — better: 「97² ＋ 2×97×3 ＋ 3²」 belongs to recognize; use kind 3 ＝ `n² − c² ＋ 2cd` is contrived. Simplest: kind 3 → 「計算 (1000−8)² 與 992² 相差多少」 is odd too. Recommend dropping kind 3 and adding 「`999² ＋ 2×999 ＋ 1`」 (和平方辨識 of a 差平方速算 number) at 挑戰.

### Unit conjugateExpand 平方差展開
- Variety: kinds 0 `(ax＋b)(ax−b)`, 1 `(ax＋by)(ax−by)`, 2 **identical to 1** (`norm` puts x first; L1005 `pa(b,a)` is re-sorted), 3 `(ax²＋by)(ax²−by)`, 4 `(x＋b)(x−b) − (x＋c)(x−c)`. Effective variety: 4. Missing: 常數在前 `(5＋x)(5−x)`, 負號型 `(−x＋3)(x＋3)`, `(2x＋3y)(3y−2x)`, 填空 `(3x＋2)(3x−□)＝9x²−4`.
- Difficulty: advanced ＝ basic forms with x²; 挑戰 fine.
- Plausibility/tip/mode: fraction `((21)/(5)x ＋ (11)/(4)y)(…)` → `(441)/(25)x² − (121)/(16)y²`; textbooks use `(½x ＋ ⅓y)`. Decimal `(1.5x ＋ 4.8)(…)＝2.25x² − 23.04` is OK-ish; prefer one-digit tenths ≤ 1.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Fix kind 2 so the rendered order is really swapped: build the factor text by hand, e.g. `(b＋a)(−b＋a)` → 「展開：(3y ＋ 2x)(−3y ＋ 2x)」, 「展開：(5 ＋ x)(5 − x)」 → `25 − x²` (answer keeps that order), 「(−2x ＋ 3)(2x ＋ 3)」 → `9 − 4x²`. Advanced level.
  2. [P2] Add 填空/反求 kind (advanced): 「(4x ＋ □)(4x − □) ＝ 16x² − 25，□＝」 answer `5`; 「若 (x＋a)(x−a)＝x²−49，a＞0，求 a」.
  3. [P2] Fraction/decimal coefficient pool for all expansion units: fraction from {½,⅓,¼,⅔,¾,⅕,⅖,3/2}, decimal from {0.1…0.9, 1.5, 2.5}; never two different denominators inside one bracket pair.

### Unit sumExpand 和平方展開
- Variety: 5 kinds (`(ax＋b)²`, `(ax＋by)²`, `(ax²＋by)²`, `(ax＋by)² − (ax)²`, `(ax＋by)²＋(ax−by)²`). Good.
- Difficulty: basic gets `(3x²＋4y)²` although levels text puts 含 x² at 挑戰; 挑戰 keeps `(5x²＋y)²`. Shift: basic {0,1}, advanced {1,2,3}, challenge {3,4,new}.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Re-window as above.
  2. [P2] Add 常數在前/負數 kind: 「展開：(3 ＋ 2x)²」, 「(−x − 2)²」 (sign trap, answer `x² ＋ 4x ＋ 4`).
  3. [P2] Add 填空: 「x² ＋ 10x ＋ □ 是完全平方式，□＝」 → 25; 「(x ＋ □)² ＝ x² ＋ 8x ＋ 16」. (Also serves factor topic.)
  4. [P3] 應用: 「正方形邊長 (x＋3) 公分，面積為？」 basic; 「邊長 x 的正方形每邊加 3，面積增加多少」 advanced (`6x＋9`).

### Unit differenceExpand 差平方展開
- Variety: 5 kinds incl. `(−ax＋by)²` and `(a−b)² − (a＋b)(a−b)`; good.
- Difficulty: similar window issue but less harmful. Basic `(−3x ＋ 3y)²` is fine given the tip.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Same coefficient-pool rule for fraction/decimal (`(−(14)/(3)x ＋ (7)/(2)y)²` is not printable material).
  2. [P2] Add `(b − ax)²` with constant first: 「展開：(2 − 3x)²」 → `9x² − 12x ＋ 4` (and accept `4 − 12x ＋ 9x²` in teacher copy).

### Unit threeTerm 三項式平方
- Variety: 5 kinds (`(x＋y＋c)²`, `(ax＋by＋cz)²`, `(ax−by＋c)²`, `(ax−by−cz)²`, `(a＋b＋c)² − (a＋b)²`).
- Difficulty: basic already `(3x − 4y ＋ 2)²`, `(4x＋4y＋2z)²` (6-term answers); levels text places 三項式 at 進階. Basic should be `(x＋y＋1)²`, `(x＋2y＋3)²` with unit coefficients.
- Plausibility: decimal `(3.5x − 3.1y ＋ 4.2)²` → `29.4x`, `−26.04y` is unrealistic; fraction similar.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Basic kinds: only coefficient-1 variables with small constant; advanced: one non-unit coefficient; challenge: current kinds 3/4.
  2. [P2] Restrict fraction/decimal to a single non-integer term (e.g. `(x ＋ y ＋ ½)²`, `(2x − y ＋ 0.5)²`).

### Unit sumRecognize 和平方辨識
- Variety: kinds 0/2 hidden `2ab` (`求值：29² ＋ 928 ＋ 16²`), 1 explicit `2 × 33 × 9`, 3 `a＋b, ab → a²＋b²`, 4 `→ (a−b)²`. Kinds 3/4 ignore mode. `pn2` allows a＝b (`8² ＋ 128 ＋ 8²`).
- Difficulty: basic is 2/3 hidden-form (harder) and advanced has the explicit form (easier) — levels text says the opposite (「明寫公式」 at 進階). Fraction hidden form (`(6½)² ＋ (13¼)² ＋ 172¼`) is very hard for anyone.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Order kinds {explicit, hidden, hidden-reordered, a²＋b², (a−b)²}; basic {0,1}, advanced {1,2,3}, challenge {3,4,new}. Reject a＝b.
  2. [P2] In fraction/decimal, keep 2ab explicit or make b an integer so 2ab is obvious (`(6½)² ＋ 2 × 6½ × 3½ ＋ (3½)²`).
  3. [P2] New 挑戰 kinds: 「已知 a ＋ 1/a ＝ 3，求 a² ＋ 1/a²」 (ans 7); 「已知 x²＋y²＝25、xy＝12，求 (x＋y)²」.
  4. [P3] Tip for kinds 3/4 is off (「看出 a²＋2ab＋b²」); append 「；a²＋b²＝(a＋b)²−2ab」.

### Unit differenceRecognize 差平方辨識
- Variety: mirrors sumRecognize; kind 4 `a＋b, a−b → ab` is a good 挑戰 form. `a−b＝0、ab＝25` degenerate.
- Difficulty/mode: same issues as sumRecognize.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Same reordering/mode rule; reject `a−b＝0`.
  2. [P3] Add 「已知 a−b＝3、ab＝4，求 (a＋b)²」 (ans 25).

### Unit formulaChain 公式連鎖化簡
- Variety: 5 kinds (3-factor chain with `x`, `ax`, `ax/by`; 4-factor `x⁸` chain; `(a＋b)(a−b) − (a−b)²`). Good.
- Difficulty: fine in integer mode (4-factor only from advanced).
- Plausibility/mode: **fraction/decimal are absurd**: `((7)/(2)x ＋ (27)/(5))(…)((49)/(4)x² ＋ (729)/(25))` → `(2401)/(16)x⁴ − (531441)/(625)`; `(x ＋ 3.9)(x − 3.9)(x² ＋ 15.21)` → `x⁴ − 231.3441`. 4-factor kind silently falls back to integers.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] In fraction/decimal mode restrict b to {½, ⅓, 0.5, 0.1, 0.2} with a＝1 (`(x ＋ ½)(x − ½)(x² ＋ ¼)` → `x⁴ − 1/16`), or set `modes:["integer"]` for this unit.
  2. [P2] Add the classic 「(2＋1)(2²＋1)(2⁴＋1)(2⁸＋1)」 numeric chain → `2¹⁶ − 1` at 挑戰 (會考型).

### Unit comprehensive 綜合計算
- Variety: 5 kinds, all 「湊成公式」; kind 3 `u² ＋ 2uw ＋ w²` always sums to 50/100 → answers 2500/10000 repeat 4–5 times per sheet.
- Difficulty: 挑戰 `7² ＋ 2×7×43 ＋ 43²` is easy; no real step-up.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Vary `u＋w ∈ {20,30,40,50,60,80,100,200}`; add negative middle (`47² − 2×47×7 ＋ 7²`).
  2. [P2] 挑戰 kinds: 「1999 × 2001 ＋ 1」 (＝2000²), 「(2024² − 2023²) ÷ 4047」, 「1.25² × 4 − 0.75² × 4」.
  3. [P3] Fraction answers as 帶分數 (see nearProduct).

## Topic: polyops — 多項式四則
- Levels promised: 基礎「一、二次式合併與加減、單層括號、單項式乘除、除以常數或 x、一次式相乘、x−a 整除」／進階「三次式、三個多項式、雙層括號、單項式平方、多項式乘單項式、有餘式、uA−vB」／挑戰「化簡後求值、反求、三層括號、(axᵐ)³、二次式除以二次式、整除求 k、AB−C、含除法混合」.
- Overall: 13 units × 5 kinds, the kinds themselves are well chosen and textbook-faithful in integer mode. Problems: (a) combine never shuffles; (b) sliding window puts 3-level nesting and cubic ÷ (−4x＋4) in basic; (c) fraction/decimal modes multiply several arbitrary non-integers (denominators 60–800, 3-decimal coefficients); (d) no 降冪/係數/次數 reading, no 反求係數, no 應用.

### Unit combine 合併同類項
- Variety: 5 kinds by degree + 「次數與常數項」 + 「求值」. **Bug:** L1096 `shuffle(ex)` result is discarded, so every question is `c ＋ c ＋ cx ＋ cx ＋ cx² ＋ cx²` (sample: `−7 ＋ 5 − 5x ＋ 2x − 6x² ＋ 3x²`) — 同類項 already adjacent, and always ascending.
- Difficulty: basic includes 3rd degree (levels says 一、二次); 挑戰 shares plain-cubic with basic.
- Plausibility/mode: fraction mixes denominators 2,3,4,5 in one line → LCD 60 (`(69)/(10)x² − (37)/(6)x ＋ (35)/(6)`); acceptable at advanced, too heavy for basic.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] `ex=shuffle(ex)`; also allow 3 terms of one degree at advanced.
  2. [P2] Per-level: basic {deg1, deg2 shuffled}, advanced {deg2, deg3, 次數/常數項}, challenge {deg3, 求值, new}. Basic fraction: one denominator per question (all halves or all thirds).
  3. [P2] New basic kind 「將 3x − 5 ＋ 2x² 按 x 的降冪排列，並寫出 x 項係數與常數項」 → `2x² ＋ 3x − 5；係數 3，常數項 −5`; 挑戰 kind 「化簡後求 x＝−½ 時的值」.
  4. [P3] Kind 「下列何者為多項式」 (判斷題: `1/x ＋ 2`, `√2 x`, `x² ＋ 1/3`) — 108課綱 1-2 概念.

### Unit addition 多項式加法
- Variety: 5 kinds incl. three-term sum and 反求 `(3x²＋2) ＋（ ）＝ 10x² − 3`; good.
- Difficulty: OK; basic gets 3 polys (fine).
- Proposed changes: [P3] Add 文字題 wording variant 「A＝…，B＝…，求 A 與 B 的和」 (overlaps definedOps; optional). Otherwise good; no change.

### Unit subtraction 多項式減法
- Variety: 5 kinds incl. `a − [b − (c)]` and 「某多項式減去…得…」; good.
- Difficulty: fine.
- Proposed changes: [P3] Add the reverse wording 「減去某多項式得…，求此多項式」 (answer `a − c`, the common sign trap). Otherwise good.

### Unit nested 多層括號化簡
- Variety: 5 kinds (`a − k[b − (c)]`, same with quadratics, 3-level, `k1(a) − k2[b − k3(c)]`, `k{a − [b − (c − d)]}`); good.
- Difficulty: basic gets the 3-level kind (`−2x² − 3x − [x² ＋ 4x − (−x ＋ 4 − (−3x − 1))]`); levels says 雙層 at 進階, 三層 at 挑戰.
- Plausibility/mode: **fraction/decimal multipliers** (`(13)/(5)[…]`, `2.3[…]`, `1.7(…) − 2.6[… − 0.4(…)]`) give `0.294x² − 0.55x ＋ 0.042`, `−(261)/(50)x`. No textbook does this.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Multipliers `k`, `k1..k3` must be integers (or ½) in every mode; only the inner polynomials carry fractions/decimals.
  2. [P2] Per-level: basic {kind0 linear, kind1 quadratic}, advanced {1,2,3}, challenge {3,4}.

### Unit monoMultiply 單項式乘法
- Variety: 5 kinds; good. Missing sign trap `(−2x)³` vs `−(2x)³`, `(−x)⁴`.
- Difficulty: 挑戰 includes `(6x³)²` (kind 2 at all levels); decimal 挑戰 `(2.6x)³ × (−1.9x²)＝−33.3944x⁵`, fraction `((11)/(3)x³)³ × ½x＝(1331)/(54)x¹⁰`.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] In fraction/decimal: cube only coefficients from {½, ⅓, 0.5, 0.2, 0.1, 2}; at most one non-integer factor per product.
  2. [P2] Re-window so `(ax)²` alone is basic only; add kind 「計算 (−2x)³ 與 −(2x)³」 / 「(−x)⁴」 at advanced.

### Unit polyTimesMono 多項式乘單項式
- Variety: 5 kinds incl. `c(p) − d(q)` and `x(p) ＋ c(q)`; good.
- Difficulty: fine.
- Plausibility/mode: decimal `(−5.7x³ − 0.1x² ＋ 2.2x)(−2.7x)` is heavy but single-multiplication; acceptable at advanced. Basic decimal/fraction should use a nicer monomial (0.5x, ½x, 2x).
- Proposed changes: [P2] Basic fraction/decimal: monomial coefficient from {½, ⅓, 0.5, 0.2, 1.5}; polynomial integer. Otherwise good.

### Unit binomialMultiply 兩多項式相乘
- Variety: kinds `(x＋a)(x＋b)`, `(ax＋b)(cx＋d)`, `k(…)(…)`, `(…)(…) ＋ cx²` (artificial), three binomials. Missing: 反求係數, 幾何面積.
- Difficulty: basic has `−4(−7x ＋ 6)(−3x ＋ 5)`; `(x − 2)(x − 2)` should be a square.
- Plausibility/mode: fraction `−(13)/(4)((22)/(5)x − (19)/(4))((3)/(2)x − (14)/(5))` → `(50557)/(800)x`; decimal `−4.4(−6.9x ＋ 5.7)(0.5x ＋ 6.8)` → `193.908x`.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Fraction/decimal: exactly one factor non-integer, `k` integer; e.g. 「展開：(½x ＋ 3)(2x − 5)」, 「展開：(0.5x − 2)(3x ＋ 4)」.
  2. [P2] Replace kind 3 (`＋ 5x²`) with 反求: 「(x ＋ 3)(x ＋ a) 展開後 x 項係數為 7，求 a 與常數項」 → `a＝4，常數項 12`.
  3. [P2] Add 應用 kind (advanced): 「長方形長 (2x ＋ 3) 公分、寬 (x ＋ 1) 公分，面積為？」 → `2x² ＋ 5x ＋ 3`.
  4. [P3] Reject a＝b in kind 0.

### Unit polyMultiply 多項式乘法進階
- Variety: 5 kinds (lin×quad, `(ax²＋b)(cx²＋d)`, quad×lin, quad×quad, `(lin)²×lin`); good, 缺項 handled.
- Difficulty: 挑戰 shares quad×lin with basic; fine otherwise.
- Plausibility/mode: fraction quad×quad → denominators 200/675 (`(10768)/(675)x`); decimal `(3.6x ＋ 3.6)²(−1.5x ＋ 2.8)`.
- Proposed changes: [P1] Same one-non-integer-factor rule; quad×quad integer-only. [P2] Re-window (basic {0,2}, advanced {1,2,3}, challenge {3,4}).

### Unit monoDivide 單項式除法
- Variety: 5 kinds incl. double division and `(ax^e)² ÷ bx^f`; good.
- Difficulty: fine. Integer mode can give fractional answers (`(4x²)² ÷ (−3x²)＝−(16)/(3)x²`) — textbook-legit, keep.
- Plausibility/mode: decimal dividends with 3 decimals (`−3.344x⁶ ÷ 1.1x² ÷ 1.6x`); fraction `(1089)/(40)x⁶`.
- Proposed changes: [P2] Decimal/fraction: divisor coefficients from {0.5, 0.2, 0.4, 1.5, 2.5, ½, ⅓, ¼}, quotient ≤ 1 decimal, so the dividend has ≤ 2 decimals.

### Unit polyDivideMono 多項式除以單項式
- Variety: 5 kinds (÷ const, ÷ cx, ÷ cx², with remainder, 反求被除式); good.
- Difficulty: basic always 餘式 0 — acceptable (concept), advanced adds remainder.
- Plausibility/mode: fraction divisor `−(2)/(3)x` with dividend `(46)/(15)x³`; realistic fraction form is integer dividend ÷ integer monomial giving fractional quotient.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Fraction mode: integer dividend/divisor, quotient fractional: 「求商與餘式：(6x³ − 4x² ＋ 3x) ÷ (2x)」 → `商 3x² − 2x ＋ 3/2；餘式 0`. Decimal mode: 「(3x² ＋ 1.5x) ÷ 0.5」, 「÷ (0.2x)」.
  2. [P3] Tip: add 「除以常數時每一項都要除」.

### Unit longDivision 多項式長除法
- Variety: 5 kinds (exact ÷ (x＋a), remainder, ÷ (ax＋b), ÷ 二次式, 整除求 k [integer only]). Missing 反求被除式 for linear divisor.
- Difficulty: basic gets kind 2 with divisor `(−4x ＋ 4)` (negative leading coefficient, common factor 4) and a cubic — not a textbook form and too hard for 基礎 (levels says 「x−a 的整除」). ÷ 二次式 (`x² ＋ 2x − 4`) is 高一 content; keep 挑戰 only and say so in levels text.
- Plausibility/mode: **fraction/decimal mode unprintable**: `((11)/(5)x³ − (6)/(25)x² − (72)/(25)x − 5/2) ÷ (x − 6/5)`, `(2.6x³ − 0.24x² − 7.36x ＋ 5.44) ÷ (x − 1.4)`. In fraction/decimal 挑戰 kind 4 returns null so only kinds 2/3 appear.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Kind 2: divisor `ax＋b` with `a∈{2,3}`, `gcd(a,b)＝1`, `a＞0`; basic restricted to kinds 0/1.
  2. [P1] Fraction mode ＝ integer dividend & non-monic divisor giving fractional quotient (the real textbook case): 「(x² ＋ 3x ＋ 1) ÷ (2x − 1)」 → `商 ½x ＋ 7/4；餘式 11/4`. Decimal mode: divisor `(x ＋ 0.5)` or `(2x − 1)` with ≤ 1-decimal quotient.
  3. [P2] Add 「已知多項式除以 (x − 2) 得商 x² ＋ 3x − 1、餘式 4，求此多項式」 at advanced.
  4. [P2] Levels text: mark 「二次式除以二次式」 as 延伸.

### Unit definedOps 代入 A、B、C 運算
- Variety: `A＋B`, `uA − vB`, `uA − vB ＋ wC`, `AB`, `AB − C`. Always 「−B」; no `A − B`, `2A ＋ B`, `A − 2B` plain.
- Difficulty: basic has `3A − B ＋ 2C` (3 polys, fine). 挑戰 OK.
- Plausibility: wording after the `<div>` lacks a connector — prints as 「設 A＝…，B＝…\n求 A＋B。」 OK on page.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Randomise sign and let u＝1: `A − B`, `2A ＋ B`, `A − 2B ＋ C`.
  2. [P3] Add 「求 A − B 的次數與 x 項係數」 variant (basic).

### Unit mixedOps 四則混合運算
- Variety: 5 kinds mixing products, squares, ÷ cx, ÷ (x＋a); best unit in the topic.
- Difficulty: good ladder in integer mode.
- Plausibility/mode: decimal `(1.85x³ ＋ 13.69x² − 9.99x) ÷ (3.7x)`; fraction denominators 240/900.
- Proposed changes: [P2] Apply the one-non-integer-factor rule; ÷ divisor integer in fraction/decimal. Otherwise good.

## Topic: factor — 因式分解
- Levels promised: 基礎「係數 5 以內，直接觀察公因式、完全平方、平方差、首一」／進階「係數 8 以內，共同括號、分組、非首一、先提、代換；各項也可能倒序」／挑戰「係數 12 以內，配方、拆項、二元雙十字、已知一因式求另一因式、求 m」.
- Overall: generators are sound (factor-first then expand, verified), but (a) difficulty within a unit is only `levelMax` 5/8/12 (挑戰 ＝ bigger numbers) plus the 「求另一因式」 wrapper; (b) the reversed-order wrapper is applied at **basic** too (`pick([[0,1],[0,1],[0,1,2]])` L1245) contrary to the levels text; (c) fraction/decimal ＝ always `k[integer problem]` with k from `scaleFor`, never a fraction inside a factor; (d) several canonical 國二 forms are missing (完全平方式求 k, 二元齊次, 變號共同括號, 三一分組); (e) splitTerms/multivariable/completing are 高一 content presented as regular units.

### Unit factorCheck 因式／倍式判定
- Variety: 3 wrappers (因式 yes/no + 商, 倍式 wording, 求 m); 58% yes. Good.
- Difficulty: ladder by wrapper, fine. 「否」 answers give no remainder.
- Plausibility/mode: fraction/decimal (`判斷 (4x ＋ 3) 是否為 (64)/(5)x² ＋ (128)/(5)x ＋ (64)/(5) 的因式`) is not a real form.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] `modes:["integer"]` for this unit (or fraction mode ＝ 「(2x − 1) 是否為 x² ＋ ½x − ½ 的因式」 with integer k).
  2. [P3] 「否」 → 「否，餘式為 …」 in teacher copy (remainder already computed).

### Unit commonMonomial 提出單項公因式
- Variety: single form `x^m(ax＋b)` scaled by k; reversed order at all levels. Missing 二元 `3x²y − 6xy²`, 三項 `2x³ − 4x² ＋ 6x`, 負首項 「−x² ＋ 3x ＝ −x(x − 3)」 as deliberate kind.
- Difficulty: 挑戰 only differs by `m≤3` and 12-coefficients.
- Plausibility/mode: fraction `−(3)/(2)x ＋ 2x²＝½x(4x − 3)` is a legit 參考書 form; decimal `1.5x² − 3.75x＝0.75x(2x − 5)` has no canonical answer (also `1.5x(x − 2.5)`); acceptable but note in teacher copy.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Kinds: basic `x(ax＋b)` k＝1 mostly; advanced add 三項 `kx^m(ax² ＋ bx ＋ c)` and 二元 `axy(…)`; challenge 「−」 first and 二元 `6x²y³ − 9x³y²`.
  2. [P2] Reversed-order wrapper off at basic (`[[0],[0,1],[0,1,2]]`).

### Unit commonBracket 提出共同括號
- Variety: one form `(A)(F) ＋ c(F)` (optionally `k[…]`), plus 「求另一因式」 on the expanded quadratic at 挑戰 (which no longer exercises 共同括號). Missing the two standard forms: two products `(x−1)(x＋2) ＋ (x−1)(3x＋4)` and the sign-flip `x(x−2) − 3(2−x)`, `(a−b)² ＋ (b−a)`.
- Difficulty: flat.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Add sign-flip kind (advanced): 「因式分解：x(x − 2) − 3(2 − x)」 → `(x − 2)(x ＋ 3)`; 「(x − 3)² ＋ 2(3 − x)」 → `(x − 3)(x − 5)`.
  2. [P2] Add two-product kind (basic/advanced): 「(2x ＋ 1)(x − 4) ＋ (2x ＋ 1)(x ＋ 3)」 → `(2x ＋ 1)(2x − 1)`.
  3. [P3] 挑戰 wrapper for this unit should keep bracket structure: 「已知 (x − 5) 是 (3x − 1)(x − 5) ＋ 5(x − 5) 的因式，另一因式為」.

### Unit grouping 分組分解
- Variety: only `ax² ＋ bx ＋ cx ＋ d` (quadratic four-term). Missing `ax ＋ ay ＋ bx ＋ by`, `x² − xy ＋ 3x − 3y`, `x³ ＋ x² ＋ x ＋ 1`, 三一分組 `x² − 2x ＋ 1 − y²`.
- Difficulty: 挑戰 ＝ bigger numbers + 「求另一因式」.
- Plausibility: can emit cancelling middle terms (`98x² − 84x ＋ 84x − 72`, line 4862; fraction `− (35)/(4)x ＋ (35)/(4)x`), and repeated factors printed `0.5(x − 4)(x − 4)`.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Reject `A.a*B.b ＋ A.b*B.a ＝ 0` and `A ≡ B` (print `(x − 4)²` if kept).
  2. [P1] Add 二元 kinds: basic 「因式分解：ax ＋ ay ＋ 2x ＋ 2y」→`(a ＋ 2)(x ＋ y)`; advanced 「x² ＋ xy − 3x − 3y」; challenge 三一分組 「x² − y² − 2x ＋ 1」→`(x − 1 ＋ y)(x − 1 − y)` and 「a² − b² ＋ a − b」.
  3. [P2] Reversed-order wrapper off at basic.

### Unit squareSum 和的完全平方
- Variety: `k(ax＋b)²`; with reversal and 「求另一因式」 (which for a square gives the same factor — `已知 (10x ＋ 9) 是 100x² ＋ 180x ＋ 81 的一個因式，求另一個因式 → (10x ＋ 9)`, a strange question). Missing 二元 `x² ＋ 4xy ＋ 4y²`, 括號型 `(x＋y)² ＋ 6(x＋y) ＋ 9`, and 完全平方式求 k.
- Difficulty: 挑戰 is `2(4x ＋ 11)²` — bigger numbers only.
- Plausibility/mode: fraction/decimal only as `k[…]`; never `x² ＋ x ＋ ¼ ＝ (x ＋ ½)²`, `x² ＋ 0.6x ＋ 0.09`.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Add 「若 x² ＋ kx ＋ 25 是完全平方式，求 k」 (ans `±10`) and 「x² ＋ 10x ＋ k 是完全平方式，k＝」 (ans 25) — advanced/challenge; 會考 standard.
  2. [P1] Fraction/decimal mode: 70% factor-internal fractions `(x ＋ ½)²`, `(2x ＋ ⅓)²`, `(x ＋ 0.3)²`, 30% current `k[…]`.
  3. [P2] 二元 kind `(ax ＋ by)²` (advanced), 括號代換 `(x ＋ y)² ＋ 2(x ＋ y) ＋ 1` (challenge). Drop the 「求另一因式」 wrapper for perfect squares (replace by 「求 k」 kind).
  4. [P2] Reversed order off at basic.

### Unit squareDiff 差的完全平方
- Same structure and issues as squareSum (`已知 (3x − 4) 是 9x² − 24x ＋ 16 的一個因式 → (3x − 4)`).
- Proposed changes: [P1]/[P2] mirror squareSum (fraction `(x − ½)²`, `求 k` with sign, 二元 `(2x − 3y)²`, no self-factor wrapper).

### Unit differenceSquares 平方差公式
- Variety: `k(ax＋b)(ax−b)` only. Reversed order prints `−16 ＋ 25x²` (unnatural; textbooks write `16 − 25x²` ＝ `(4＋5x)(4−5x)`). Missing 二元 `4x² − 9y²`, 括號型 `(x＋1)² − 4`, `(2x＋1)² − (x−3)²`, 連續平方差 `x⁴ − 16`, 常數在前 `9 − x²`.

- Plausibility/mode: fraction `(175)/(4)x² − (63)/(4)`, never `x² − 1/9`, `0.25x² − 1`.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Fraction/decimal: `x² − ¼`, `4x² − 1/9`, `x² − 0.09`, `0.25x² − 4` (k＝1), keep `k[…]` as minority.
  2. [P1] Add 二元 `a²x² − b²y²` (basic/advanced) and 常數在前 `b² − a²x²` → `(b ＋ ax)(b − ax)` as a real kind instead of the `−16 ＋ 25x²` reversal.

### Unit monicTrinomial 首一二次三項式
- Variety: `k(x＋r)(x＋s)`; basic has k≠1 in 5 of 8 samples (`3(x − 1)(x ＋ 2)`), which is 先提公因式, not 首一 observation. Missing 二元齊次 `x² ＋ 5xy ＋ 6y²`, 「x² ＋ kx ＋ 12 可分解，求所有整數 k」, 「x² ＋ 7x ＋ k」.
- Difficulty: 挑戰 ＝ |r|,|s| ≤ 12 and the wrapper; adequate but add structure.
- Plausibility/mode: fraction `(4)/(5)x² − (8)/(5)x − (32)/(5)` only; a realistic fraction case is `x² ＋ (5/6)x ＋ 1/6 ＝ (x ＋ ½)(x ＋ ⅓)` (挑戰 only).

### Unit splitTerms 分項拆項分解
- Variety: `k(x² ＋ bx ＋ c)(x² ＋ d)` quartic only (`x⁴ ＋ 2x³ ＋ 13x² ＋ 18x ＋ 36`).

- Proposed changes:

### Unit multivariable 二元雙十字交乘

## Cross-cutting implementation notes
- Replace the shared window `pick([[0,1,2],[1,2,3],[2,3,4]][Lv])` in both `mkUnit`s with per-unit `levels:[[…],[…],[…]]` arrays so each unit can declare its own ladder (needed by most proposals above).
- Factor `unit()` L1245: use `[[0],[0,1],[0,1,2]]` so reversal starts at 進階 as the levels text says; and skip the 「求另一因式」 wrapper when the factor list is a single square.
- Fraction/decimal rule for all expansion/multiplication/division kinds (mulformula, polyops): at most one non-integer factor per product; bracket multipliers integer; coefficient pools fraction {½,⅓,¼,⅔,¾,⅕,⅖,3/2,5/2}, decimal {0.1–0.9, 1.5, 2.5}; division built from integer dividend/divisor with fractional quotient.
- Units whose fraction/decimal modes add nothing and should be `modes:["integer"]`: formulaChain (unless restricted as above), factorCheck, grouping, completing, splitTerms, multivariable.
- `generate()` already reports 「數型不適用而略過」, so narrowing `modes` is safe for the UI.
