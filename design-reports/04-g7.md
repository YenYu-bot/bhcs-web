Reviewed state: branch `fix/math-answer-errors`, `/home/claude/yenyu-bot/bhcs-web/tools/math/g7-drills.html` (signedConfig L562–744, exponentConfig L746–890, scinot ~L1035, factors ~L1040–1175, linear L1180–1403, simultaneous ~L1404–1545, inequality ~L1546–2071). Curriculum checks against a 113學年 國一 108課綱 課程計畫 (chc.edu.tw PDF): 零次方 **is** in N-7-6; 負指數科學記號 **is** in N-7-8; 輾轉相除法 is **not** in 108 國中; 絕對值不等式 is **not** in A-7-7/8 (高一); 聯立方程式無解/無限多解 is **not** in A-7-6 (只處理恰一交點); 乘法公式 (a−1)(a+1) is 國二.

## Summary

1. **Computation quality is high, but the three-level ladder is mostly "same kinds, bigger numbers"** — and in several units it is inverted or flat: signed `div` (advanced 18÷(−2) easier than basic 63÷(−7)), signed `laws` (level has no effect at all on kind), exponent `power` (challenge's `expand` kind "8×8×8×8 寫成 aⁿ" is the easiest kind on the page), exponent `negative`/`e7`/`s1`/`e1` (kinds not level-gated), `sameExp` (challenge 5⁵×2⁵ easier than basic 8⁴×125⁴×3⁴).
2. **Ugly numbers are the #1 thing a teacher would notice.** Fraction-mode challenge in signed produces answers like −352(1153)/(2700), 8(151199)/(308700), 4(299)/(2187); decimal challenge gives 1280.9328, 752.58276; inequality `frac`/`bracket` *basic* already gives x≦−(465)/(76), x>−(879)/(104); scinot `addsub` challenge gives 1.8273918×10⁻²⁰; simultaneous challenge mixes decimal coefficients with constants like −(11)/(70). None of these appear in 康軒/南一/翰林. Needs denominator/decimal caps per level.
3. **Modes are often meaningless**: `fraction`/`decimal` mode produce identical questions to `integer` in linear s3, s4(decimal), s5(decimal), e1, e4(decimal), e5(decimal), e7; simultaneous `special`, `chain`(fraction≠integer only by denominators), `subst`; inequality `gdraw/gread` (fine, by design) but `comb` decimal has no decimals. Either hide the mode toggle for those units or make it do something.
4. **Trivial/degenerate items slip into print**: `6＝x`, `x＝−6` (e1), `(5x＋6)＝−4` (e3 challenge), `8x＋x＝` (s1 challenge), `7⁰＋12⁰＋62⁰＝3` six times in one 零次方 block, `((5)/(6))⁴×((6)/(5))⁴＝1` five of eight in sameExp advanced-fraction.
5. **Curriculum drift** to flag on the sheet or gate behind challenge: exponent `smart` kind `square` (201²−200×202 needs (a−1)(a+1), 國二); simultaneous `subst` basic uses 1/(x＋y) reciprocals (分式, beyond 國中 — the 108-level 替代法 is linear combos of (x＋y),(x−y)); inequality `abs` whole unit (高一); factors `euclid` (not in 108, still common in 參考書 — keep but label 「補充」); linear s2/s3 advanced uses `y` terms in a 一元一次式 unit.
6. **Standard textbook question types missing** (the page is a 計算 drill, so these are P2 unless noted): 反求 types everywhere (「x＝2 是 3x＋a＝10 的解，求 a」, 「若 2ˣ＝8 求 x」 exists but 「(a−2)x＝5 無解求 a」 absent, 「已知 (a,b)＝6、〔a,b〕＝72、a＝18 求 b」), 代入求值 (式子的值 — first lesson of the 一元一次式 chapter, absent), 整數解 / 最大整數解 for inequalities (會考 staple, absent), 科學記號比較大小 / 應用 (absent), 因數個數 from 標準分解式 (absent), 應用題 for 一元一次 / 聯立 / 不等式 (absent — by design, but a separate 「應用題」 topic like g8 `quadapps` would be the natural home).
7. Tips (提示) are correct and complete; two mismatches: signed `mul` tip says 「分數先約分再乘」 but the generator never builds cancellable fractions; exponent `smart` tip explains x²−xy＝x(x−y) but half the items are (a−1)(a+1).

## Topic: signed — 正負數四則運算
- Levels promised: 基礎「兩數運算、三數加減與乘除混合、簡單四則與去括號、分配律」/ 進階「三到四個數、含括號的乘除、四則混合四種版型、多重括號、結合律與分配律」/ 挑戰「四到五個數、四則混合六種版型且含乘方、五種多重括號版型、更大的數與分母」.
- Overall: integer mode is solid and matches the promise. Fraction and decimal modes are the weak spot: denominators at challenge come from {3…27} with no cap on the combined denominator, decimal products are not rounded, and the ladder for the four single-operation units is purely magnitude. No 應用題/數線/判斷 types at all (acceptable for a 計算 page, but 「反求」 and 「填入運算符號」 would cost little).

### Unit add 加法
- Variety: 1 kind (2-term; 3-term at advanced/challenge). All levels: same form, bigger |n|. Contexts n/a.
- Difficulty: basic ok (≤30). advanced/challenge differ only by magnitude; challenge fraction answers −1(181)/(300), 2(37)/(396), −5(7)/(540) are unreasonable.
- Plausibility/tip/mode: tip fine. Decimal advanced/challenge mixes 1- and 2-decimal numbers (129.6＋(−123.23)) — acceptable but 3-digit decimals (−163.2＋158.66＋(−126.82)) are not textbook.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Cap fraction answers: at basic LCM of denominators ≤ 12 and answer denominator ≤ 12; advanced ≤ 24; challenge ≤ 60. Sample challenge: `(−5(5)/(12))＋3(7)/(18)＋(−(1)/(4))＝` (LCM 36).
  2. [P2] Give challenge a real kind change instead of 3-digit numbers: 「反求」 `(−17)＋□＝−5，求 □` and 「連加去括號」 `(−3)＋(−5)＋7＋(−9)＋4＝` with 5 terms, |n|≤20. Keep 3-digit numbers only in decimal/integer at ≤20%.
  3. [P3] Basic decimal: use one decimal place only (already), but keep |n|≤20 (currently up to 17.3 fine).

### Unit sub 減法
- Variety: 1 kind. Same shape all levels.
- Difficulty: magnitude-only ladder; challenge fraction −(221)/(693), 7(55)/(112) absurd.
- Plausibility/tip/mode: tip good; advanced fraction mixes an integer like 109−(−(2)/(3))−(−(1)/(2)) — good.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Same denominator cap as `add`.
  2. [P2] Challenge: add 「相反數與減法」 kind `(−8)−(−8)＝`, `0−(−7)＝` at basic, and 「反求」 `□−(−6)＝−2` at challenge; add 「兩數差的絕對值」 `|(−7)−5|＝` only if the page also does 絕對值 (it is on the `numberline` topic — skip if duplicated there).

### Unit addsub 加減混合
- Variety: 1 kind (k=3/3–4/4–5 terms). Fine for a drill.
- Difficulty: ok structurally. Challenge fraction is the single worst block on the page: `(8)/(27)−7(1)/(12)＋(−345)−(16)/(25)＝ −352(1153)/(2700)`.
- Plausibility/tip/mode: tip good (both strategies). Decimal challenge with 5 terms of 2-decimal 3-digit numbers (74.05−(−115.8)＋29.41＋(−46.22)＋136.1) is calculator work, not a 國一 drill.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Fraction: pick all denominators of one item from a family with small LCM ({2,4,8},{3,6,12},{2,3,6},{4,6,12},{5,10},{3,9},{2,5,10}); challenge may use {6,8,12,24} or {4,6,9,12,18}. Integer parts ≤ 9.
  2. [P1] Decimal: challenge ≤ 4 terms, at most two 2-decimal numbers, |n| ≤ 200.
  3. [P2] Challenge kind 「湊整」 `(−17)＋39＋(−23)＋61＝` (pairs sum to ±10/±100) to exercise 交換律/結合律 — otherwise `laws` is the only place it appears.

### Unit mul 乘法
- Variety: 1 kind (2 factors; 3–4 at advanced). Occasional 0 factor (good).
- Difficulty: integer ladder ok (9 → 12 → 25, with 25×4 patterns at challenge). Fraction challenge `(−(1)/(27))×(−9(1)/(12))×1(17)/(27)×7(6)/(11)＝4(299)/(2187)` is absurd; decimal challenge `(−5.69)×4.02×(−7)×8＝1280.9328`.
- Plausibility/tip/mode: **tip says 「分數先約分再乘」 but no item is built to cancel** — basic fraction gives (1)/(8)×(−(1)/(6))＝−(1)/(48). This is the key fit problem of the unit.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Fraction mode: construct factors so numerators cancel denominators (`(−(3)/(8))×(4)/(9)＝−(1)/(6)`, `(−2(1)/(4))×(−(8)/(15))＝1(1)/(5)`); result denominator ≤ 12 (basic) / ≤ 24. Mixed numbers only at advanced+.
  2. [P1] Decimal mode: product must have ≤ 2 decimal places (basic: 1 place × integer; advanced: 1×1; challenge: 2×1 or three factors with one 2-place), |product| ≤ 1000.
  3. [P2] Challenge integer: add 「巧乘」 forms `(−4)×17×(−25)＝`, `8×(−125)×(−3)＝`, `(−2)×(−2)×(−2)×(−2)＝` (sign counting with 4 identical factors) — currently only 25×4 appears.

### Unit div 除法
- Variety: 1 kind (2 numbers; 3 at advanced/challenge ~30%).
- Difficulty: **inverted** — advanced integer draws quotient 1..12 and divisor 2..12, so `18÷(−2)`, `15÷(−3)`, `16÷(−4)` appear in advanced while basic has `63÷(−7)`; challenge has `40÷(−2)`. Decimal ladder is flat (basic 16.8÷(−7), challenge 62.1÷(−9)). Fraction advanced `3÷5(8)/(9)÷(−3(4)/(5))＝−(135)/(1007)`.
- Plausibility/tip/mode: tip good (covers 0 and 除數不為 0, but no item uses 0÷n — add one at basic).
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Integer: basic divisor 2–9, quotient 2–9; advanced divisor 2–12 and quotient 10–25 (two-digit quotient) or 3-number chains; challenge divisor 11–25 or chains with brackets `(−1008)÷〔9×(−8)〕`. Enforce |dividend| strictly increasing by level.
  2. [P1] Fraction: divisor chosen so the reciprocal cancels (`(−(5)/(6))÷(10)/(9)＝−(3)/(4)`); mixed-number divisor only at advanced+; result denominator ≤ 24.
  3. [P1] Decimal: challenge should be decimal÷decimal (`(−5.4)÷0.6`, `3.75÷(−1.5)`), not decimal÷integer again. Tip mentions 乘倒數 — decimal÷decimal exercises it.
  4. [P2] Basic: include `0÷(−7)＝0` ~10% and 「反求」 `(−54)÷□＝6` at challenge.

### Unit muldiv 乘除混合
- Variety: chains of 3–4 with optional bracket `a÷〔b×c〕` at advanced+. Good.
- Difficulty: structure ok. Basic decimal `1.7÷8÷(−4)＝−0.053125` and basic fraction `−(190)/(1271)` are P1 bugs (no cleanliness check for fraction/decimal chains — `okValue` only exists in `four`).
- Plausibility/tip/mode: tip good.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Apply an `okValue`-style check to muldiv: decimal results ≤ 2 places; fraction results denominator ≤ 24 and every intermediate quotient simple.
  2. [P2] Basic fraction: 3 numbers where at least one is an integer and all cancel: `(−(2)/(3))×6÷(−(4)/(5))＝5`.

### Unit four 四則混合
- Variety: 2/4/6 templates by level; power injected at challenge (35%). Good design, matches the levels text.
- Difficulty: basic already starts with `a−b×〔c−d〕` (two-level bracket) — fine but template 1 (`a×b±c÷d`) should dominate basic (currently 50/50). Fraction advanced `(3347)/(4680)`, challenge `8(151199)/(308700)`; decimal challenge `752.58276`.
- Plausibility/tip/mode: tip ok. Challenge exponent only `2^2`, `(−2)^3` with |base|≤4 — fine.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Fraction/decimal: same caps (fraction: all denominators in one family, result denominator ≤ 36; decimal: result ≤ 2 places). Reject and re-draw otherwise (there is already a 400-try loop).
  2. [P2] Basic pool weighting 70% template 1, 30% template 2; advanced adds the bracket templates.
  3. [P3] Challenge: ensure at least 2 of 8 printed items contain a power (currently random 35% can yield 0).

### Unit brackets 去多重括號
- Variety: 2 templates at basic, 5 at advanced/challenge. Challenge differs from advanced only by magnitude (3-digit).
- Difficulty: basic already contains `{…〔…〕}` 3-level items (template 3 is in the basic slice `tmpl.slice(1,3)` → index 2 is `a−〔(b+c)−(d−e)〕`, ok; but sample shows `22−{(−22)＋(−17)−〔25−(−19)〕}` at basic — template 4 leaked? `slice(1,3)` gives templates 1,2 (0-based) = 〔a+b〕−〔c−d〕 and a−〔(b+c)−(d−e)〕; the `{}` appears because `wrap` escalates to `{}` at depth 2 — fine). Challenge fraction `6(31711)/(41580)`.
- Plausibility/tip/mode: tip good (both methods). 
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Fraction cap as above.
  2. [P2] Make challenge a distinct form rather than 3-digit numbers: `−{−〔−(−a)〕}` sign-only chains (`−〔−(−7)〕＝`, `−{−〔−(−3)−5〕}＝`) and 「判斷」 `去括號後 −(a−b＋c) 等於 (A) −a−b＋c (B) −a＋b−c …` (one 選擇 per sheet).
  3. [P3] Keep 3-digit numbers out of brackets entirely (|n| ≤ 60); the skill is sign handling, not addition.

### Unit laws 計算定律
- Variety: integer kinds dist/dist2/assocMul/assocAdd; fraction dist/dist2/distFrac; decimal dist/dist2. **Level has no effect** (`kind` not gated by `CTX.diff`; `a` always from the same list). Basic `98×127＋98×73` vs advanced `25×98＋25×2` vs challenge `25×54＋25×46` — flat/inverted.
- Difficulty: flat.
- Plausibility/tip/mode: `distFrac` with d∈{57,123} → `(40)/(123)×(1)/(3)＋(83)/(123)×(1)/(3)` is a joke item; `2×(−5)×2＝−20` as a 計算定律 item is trivial. Decimal `(−3.83)×3−(−3.83)×(−7)＝−38.3` ok. The most standard 國一 分配律 item — `(−(1)/(2)＋(2)/(3)−(1)/(6))×(−12)＝` and `99×(−37)＝(100−1)×(−37)` — is absent.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Gate kinds by level. Basic: `dist` with a∈{12,25,37,47} and (b,c) summing to 10/100; `assocMul` 2×5/4×25 pairs. Advanced: `dist2` (three terms), `assocAdd` with a negative, **new `distExpand`** `(−12)×((1)/(3)−(3)/(4)＋(1)/(6))＝`, **new `nearHundred`** `(−25)×99＝`, `37×101＝`. Challenge: `−8×(−125)×(−0.25)×4`, 分數 `((5)/(6)−(3)/(4)＋(1)/(8))×(−24)`, 「反向分配」 `(−7)×23＋(−7)×(−3)＋7×(−20)＝` (needs factoring with sign change), and 「填空」 `(−3)×(□＋5)＝−3×□＋(−15)`.
  2. [P1] Remove d∈{57,123} from `distFrac`; use d ≤ 13.
  3. [P2] Drop `2×(−5)×2`-style 3-factor items with |a|≤9 from `assocMul` at any level (require at least one of 25/125/0.25 pattern).

## Topic: exponent — 指數律
- Levels promised: 基礎「指數求值、五條基本規則與零次方；數字較小」/ 進階「底數轉換、兩步驟運算、簡單未知指數與大小比較」/ 挑戰「三步驟運算、乘方的乘方巢狀、負數與分數的比較、巧算與乘方加減的提公因數」.
- Overall: broad unit list (12) that covers the chapter well; `unknown`, `compare`, `convert`, `sum` are good. Problems: ladders inside several units are flat or inverted; `power` fraction mode contains sameBase items; `zero` is mind-numbingly repetitive; `smart` leans on a 國二 identity; big answers printed in full (`3^13（＝1594323）`, `6^14`) are inconsistent (threshold 10⁷ means some show value, some don't — fine, but `12^4＝20736` at basic is too heavy).

### Unit power 指數記法與求值
- Variety: integer kinds plain/negBase/negOut → negBase/negOut/pair → pair/negOut/expand. Fraction mode: pos/neg/pair/mul regardless of level, where `mul` (`((5)/(9))^3×((5)/(9))^2`) is a sameBase item with answer (3125)/(59049).
- Difficulty: **inverted** — `expand` (`8×8×8×8 用指數記法表示`) is the easiest kind on the page but is challenge-only; `12^4＝20736` at basic is too big.
- Plausibility/tip/mode: tip correct. Missing standard kinds: 「底數與指數」 identification (`(−2)^5 的底數是 __、指數是 __`), 「乘積的指數記法」 (`2×2×2×3×3＝2^□×3^□`), 「展開」 (`(−3)^4＝(−3)×(−3)×(−3)×(−3)`), 「10 的次方」 `10^5＝`.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Move `expand` to basic (b ≤ 5, e ≤ 4) and add `identify` at basic: `(−3)^4 的底數是 __，指數是 __，值是 __`。Basic `plain` b ≤ 9, e ≤ 4.
  2. [P1] Fraction mode: remove `mul` kind (belongs to sameBase); gate: basic pos/neg with e=2,3 and d ≤ 5; advanced pair; challenge e=4 or mixed-number base `(−1(1)/(2))^3＝`.
  3. [P2] Challenge: `mixedExpand` `2×2×2×5×5＝2^□×5^□`, `(−2)^3×3^2 的值`, and 「比較」 `(−2)^3 與 −2^3 相等嗎？(−2)^4 與 −2^4 呢？` (the pair kind already does this — keep one).

  2. [P2] Basic: cap m+n ≤ 8 so values stay ≤ 10⁵.
  3. [P3] Add 「填指數」 at advanced: `5^3×5^□＝5^9` — exists in `unknown`; skip if deemed duplicate.

### Unit sameExp 同指數的乘除
- Variety: integer mul/div/three → mul/div/expand → decimal/three/expand; fraction frac/fracMul/fracPow at **every** level (5 of 8 advanced-fraction items are `…×…＝1`).
- Difficulty: **inverted/flat** — challenge `5^5×2^5＝10^5` is easier than basic `8^4×125^4×3^4＝3000^4`; challenge `decimal` kind is just mul with the 2·5 pairs again.
- Plausibility/tip/mode: `mul` picks `[4,2.5]` then returns null (dead entry). Missing the actual textbook form `(a×b)^n＝a^n×b^n` applied to simplify `2^6×5^4＝2^2×10^4＝40000` (湊 10 的次方 with unequal exponents — appears nowhere).
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Re-gate: basic `mul` (2·5, 4·25), `div` (12÷4…), `expand`; advanced `three`, `frac`, **new `unequal`** `2^6×5^4＝`→`2^2×(2×5)^4＝4×10^4`; challenge **new `letter`** `(2x)^3`, `(−3a)^2`? (algebra is 國二 — instead use) **`ten`** `2^8×5^6＝□×10^□` and `0.25^4×4^4＝`, `(−(2)/(3))^5×1.5^5＝`.
  2. [P1] Fraction: drop `fracPow` (answer always 1) or limit to 1 of 8; gate `fracMul` to advanced.
  3. [P3] Remove `[4,2.5]` from `mul`.

### Unit zero 零次方
- Variety: single/sum/neg → neg/sum/expr → expr/neg/mixedSum. `sum` is the same question (`a^0＋b^0＋c^0＝3`) with different numbers — 6 of 8 basic/advanced items. Dreadful on a printed sheet.
- Difficulty: flat.
- Plausibility/tip/mode: tip ok. Missing standard kinds: 「條件」 `(x−3)^0＝1 的條件是 x≠__`, 「判斷」 `0^0 有沒有意義？`, 「混合」 `(−3)^0＋(−3)^1＋(−3)^2＝`, `2^0×2^3＝`, 「同底數除法得零次方」 `7^5÷7^5＝7^□＝__`.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Limit `sum` to ≤ 1 per block; basic kinds: single, neg, `divSame` `7^5÷7^5＝`, `mixPow` `(−3)^0＋(−3)^1＋(−3)^2`; advanced: `cond` `(x−5)^0＝1，x 不可以是多少？`, `expr`; challenge: `expr`, `mixedSum`, **`judge`** `下列何者等於 1？(A) 0^0 (B) (−1)^0 (C) −1^0 (D) 1^0` (two correct — answer 「(B)(D)」).

### Unit mixedOps 混合運算
- Variety: mdc/ten/mm → mdc/dm/ten → mdm/dm/box. Near-duplicate of `convert` (both 化同底數). `2^2×2^2＝` at basic is sameBase.

- Plausibility/tip/mode: ok.
- Proposed changes:

  2. [P3] Consider merging `convert` into `mixedOps` as its basic level (keeps unit count but clarifies the ladder) — or keep both and make `convert` purely 「寫成 a^□」 and `mixedOps` purely 「求值」.

### Unit unknown 未知指數

## Topic: linear — 一元一次式與方程式
- Levels promised: 基礎「兩項的式子、單層括號、分母 2～10；移項與等量公理、簡單解方程式、一組括號、分數型兩項」/ 進階「三項、雙層括號、含 y 的式子、代號算式；去括號五種型、分數型五種、多重括號、小數與分數混合、解的類型」/ 挑戰「三層括號、括號外有分母；解為分數、四層括號、右式不為 0、小數分母的分數型」.
- Overall: the equation units (e2–e5, e7, mix) are strong and the ladders mostly hold. Weak spots: `s1` and `e1` are flat and contain trivial items; several modes do nothing; `e6` produces artificial 「1.8＝(0.27x−0.03)/(0.3)＋…」 forms; the chapter's first lesson (代入求值 / 列式) and the 反求 types are absent; `s2` advanced uses y.

### Unit s1 式子的簡記

  2. [P3] Decimal mode: `0.5〔…〕` scalars or hide.

### Unit e6 小數分數混合
- Variety: dec=/kdec → +decD/decD2. The `decD` form `1.8＝(0.27x−0.03)/(0.3)＋(0.18x−0.21)/(0.6)＋0.09` is not a textbook form (textbooks: `(0.3x−0.2)/(0.5)＝(x＋1)/(2)`, `(x−0.5)/(0.2)−(0.3x＋1)/(0.4)＝1`); `kdec` with constants like 93.2, −0.075, 0.0875 reads as reverse-engineered.
- Difficulty: wide spread in basic (`0.1x＋1.8＝0.3x＋0.2` next to 3-fraction monsters).
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Replace `decD`/`decD2` with textbook forms: `(0.3x−0.2)/(0.5)＝(x＋1)/(2)`, `(x−0.5)/(0.2)−(0.3x＋1)/(0.4)＝1`, `(2x−1)/(0.5)＝x＋1`; choose x0 first so constants come out to ≤ 1 decimal.
  2. [P1] Basic = `dec=` only (0.1x＋1.8＝0.3x＋0.2 style, one decimal); advanced `kdec` with rhs constant ≤ 1 decimal; challenge the 小數分母 forms.

### Unit e7 方程式解的類型
- Variety: one/inf/none randomly, **not level-gated**; distribution per block is random (basic-fraction block: 7 無限多解).
- Difficulty: flat.
- Plausibility/tip/mode: modes do nothing. Missing the 反求 form: `若方程式 ax＋3＝2x＋b 無解，則 a＝__、b≠__`, `(a−2)x＝5 無解，求 a`, `若 3x＋k＝3x＋5 有無限多解，k＝__`.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Force a mix per block (≥ 2 of each type) and gate: basic one/inf/none with k ≤ 3; advanced adds fraction scalars; challenge `paramNone` `(a−2)x＝5 無解，求 a`, `paramInf` `ax＋b＝2x−3 有無限多解，求 a、b`, `paramOne` `(k＋1)x＝4 恰有一解的條件`.
  2. [P2] Hide fraction/decimal modes for e7.

### Unit mix 綜合練習
- Variety: e2/e3/e4/e5/e6/e7 — good coverage.
- Proposed changes: Good; no change beyond inherited fixes.

### Topic-level gap (linear)
- [P2] No 應用題 (年齡、數字、雞兔、工程、速率、比例分配、幾何周長). Recommend a separate topic `linearapps`（一元一次方程式應用題）as g8 does with `quadapps`; keep this page as 計算 drills. Same for simultaneous and inequality.

## Topic: simultaneous — 二元一次聯立方程式
- Levels promised: 基礎「一式已是 y＝… 或係數為 1；加減消去只需乘一次；分數型兩種；特殊係數在 50 以內」/ 進階「一般標準式、三種分數型與混合型、係數到 99、A＝B＝C 含分數、替代法三種、含括號與未化簡的標準式」/ 挑戰「解可為分數、最小公倍數較大、係數到 199、相減後有公因數的特殊型」.
- Overall: strong unit set; `special` (交錯係數) and `chain` are textbook-faithful and the worked answers (`兩式相加得 x＋y＝…`) are excellent for 教用版. Problems: challenge "fraction solutions" are implemented by injecting fraction constants into otherwise integer systems (`−4x−9y＝(7)/(4)`), which looks wrong rather than hard; `subst` is entirely a 分式 unit; `special`/`chain`/`subst` ignore modes; the 二元一次方程式 basics (解的判斷、整數解、列式) and 反求 are absent.

### Unit sub 代入消去法
- Variety: y=/x=ky/coef1 → +=lin. Variable names x,y / m,n / a,b rotate — good.
- Difficulty: basic/advanced good. Challenge integer: `{−4x−9y＝(7)/(4)；−x＝−y＋(5)/(4)}` — fraction constants in integer mode; answers x＝−(1)/(5), y＝−(7)/(5). This is how "解可為分數" was implemented; textbooks instead give integer systems whose solution happens to be fractional (`{2x＋y＝1；4x−3y＝7}` → x＝1, y＝−1… choose x0＝(1)/(2)).
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Challenge: pick x0,y0 ∈ {±1/2, ±1/3, ±3/2, ±2/3…} and build **integer-coefficient** equations around them; never put fraction constants in integer mode.
  2. [P2] Add `judge` at basic: `(2，−1) 是不是 {x＋y＝1；x−y＝3} 的解？`; `fillSolution` `x＋2y＝7 的正整數解有哪些？`.
  3. [P2] Add `reverse` at challenge: `已知 x＝2，y＝−1 是 {ax＋y＝5；x＋by＝4} 的解，求 a、b`.

### Unit elim 加減消去法
- Variety: coef/xoverd/grpfrac/mixed. Good. Fraction mode genuinely different (fraction coefficients).
- Difficulty: same fraction-constant issue at challenge (`{−3x−8y＝(147)/(5)；…}`).
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] As `sub` 1.
  2. [P2] Challenge: `bothMult` where both equations need multiplying (`{3x＋4y＝…；5x−6y＝…}` LCM 12 — present) and `threeTerm` `{2x＋3y−1＝0；…}` unsimplified (present in mix) — fine. Add `sameCoefSub` 「係數相同用相減」 explicitly at basic (present: `{x−3y＝17；3x＋3y＝3}` ok).

### Unit fracdec 分數型與小數型
- Variety: decimal coefficients, fraction coefficients, `(a＋2)/(4)＝(1)/(2)＋b` fraction-with-sum forms. Good.
- Difficulty: challenge `{−0.1a＋0.4b＝−(11)/(70)；−0.3a−1.8b＝(87)/(70)}`, `(263)/(100)` — P1 ugly (decimal coefficient with 70ths constant).
- Plausibility/tip/mode: `integer` mode of a 分數型 unit is a contradiction — it is fine as "mixed" but the mode label misleads.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Challenge: fractional solutions only with denominators 2,3,4,5 and constants that stay 1-decimal in decimal items / small fractions in fraction items.
  2. [P2] Add `numeratorSum` at advanced: `{(x＋y)/(3)＝2；(x−y)/(2)＝1}` and `(x＋1)/(2)＋(y−1)/(3)＝1` (standard).
  3. [P3] Rename modes for this unit: integer→「混合」.

### Unit special 特殊係數型
- Variety: swap/diff (交錯係數、相減有公因數). Good; answers show the trick.
- Difficulty: good ladder by coefficient size.
- Plausibility/tip/mode: fraction/decimal modes produce identical items — hide modes.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Hide modes; add `sumDiffGiven` challenge `{x＋y＝…；x−y＝…}` derived 「求 x²−y²」? (國二) — no. Instead add `addFirst` `{3x＋5y＝19；5x＋3y＝21}，求 x＋y 與 x−y 的值` (ask for the intermediate quantities only — 會考 form).

### Unit chain A式＝B式＝C式
- Variety: linear/fraction-numerator chains. Good.
- Difficulty: challenge again via fraction constants `−4x−5y−(65)/(3)＝…` — P1 as above.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Challenge via integer coefficients with fractional solution, or via three-way with brackets `2(x−1)＝3(y＋1)＝x＋y＋4`.
  2. [P3] Hide decimal mode (no decimals).

### Unit subst 替代法
- Variety: 1/(x＋y) reciprocals at **basic**; 1/(x＋4), 1/(xy) at advanced+. All three levels are 分式 systems — beyond 108 國中 (分式 is 高一). The 國一 替代法 is `{2(x＋y)＋3(x−y)＝7；3(x＋y)−(x−y)＝5}` (substitute a＝x＋y, b＝x−y).
- Difficulty: flat (same reciprocal form at every level).
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Basic: linear-combination substitution `{2(x＋y)＋3(x−y)＝7；3(x＋y)−(x−y)＝5}` and `{3(x＋2)−2(y−1)＝…}`; advanced: `{(x＋y)/(2)＋(x−y)/(3)＝…}`; challenge: keep the reciprocal forms but label the unit 「替代法（含分式，補充）」 or gate reciprocals to challenge only.
  2. [P2] Modes do nothing — hide.

### Unit mix 綜合練習
- Variety: grp/unsimp/sub/elim/fracdec/chain/special/subst. Good.
- Proposed changes: inherits the above; [P3] cap `subst` share to ≤ 1 per block.

### Topic-level gaps (simultaneous)
- [P2] 應用題 (separate topic recommended). [P3] 無解/無限多解 systems are **not** in 108 國中 (A-7-6 handles only one intersection) — do not add; if the owner wants them for 參考書 coverage, make it an off-by-default 「補充」 unit.

## Topic: inequality — 一元一次不等式
- Levels promised: 基礎「ax＋b 與常數比較、一組括號、分數型兩種、小數型一種、a＜b＜c 與簡單聯立、整數端點的數線、|ax＋b|≦k」/ 進階「兩邊都有 x、兩組括號、四種分數型、括號與小數混合、括號的合併形式、分數與小數端點、絕對值四種不等號」/ 挑戰「三組括號含分數常數、複雜分數型、分數與小數混合的括號、分數的合併形式、分數係數的絕對值、含常數項與分母的絕對值」.
- Overall: the number-line units (gdraw/gread/sgraph) and `comb` are very good and unusual for a generator. The core defect is **solution ugliness**: from `basic`-advanced upward, answers are routinely x≧−(246)/(31), x≦(840)/(43), x>−(879)/(104). 康軒/南一 inequalities almost always solve to integers or halves/thirds; the generator picks coefficients first and never checks the boundary. Second: `abs` is 高一 material. Third: the 會考 staples 「整數解」 and 應用題 are absent.

### Unit basic 基本型（不等式性質）
- Variety: `ax＋b ◇ c` → both sides x. Fine.
- Difficulty: basic integer answers integral (good); fraction mode at basic already `x<−42`, `x<30`; advanced fraction `x>210`, `x≦(840)/(43)`.
- Plausibility/tip/mode: tip good. The unit name says 不等式性質 but no property question exists (`若 a>b，則 −3a □ −3b`, 「判斷：兩邊同乘 −1 要變號」).
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Choose the boundary x₀ first (integer at basic; half/third at advanced; small fraction den ≤ 6 at challenge), then build coefficients so |constants| ≤ 60.
  2. [P2] Add `property` at basic: `已知 a＜b，在 □ 填入 ＜ 或 ＞：(1) a＋5 □ b＋5 (2) −2a □ −2b (3) (a)/(3) □ (b)/(3)`; `judge` `x＝2 是不是 3x−1＞4 的解？`.
  3. [P2] Add `intSol` at advanced/challenge: `求 2x−3＜7 的最大整數解`, `滿足 −5≦2x＋1＜4 的整數 x 有幾個？` (會考 staple), `正整數解有幾個`.

### Unit bracket 括號型
- Variety: 1/2/3 brackets. Good.
- Difficulty: challenge fraction/decimal answers `x>−(879)/(104)`, `x≧−(251)/(182)`, `x<−(144)/(113)` — P1.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Same boundary-first construction; challenge boundary denominators ≤ 6.
  2. [P3] Challenge: `bothSidesBracket` with fraction scalars is present; fine.

### Unit frac 分數型
- Variety: fraction coefficients / fraction numerators / mixed. Good.
- Difficulty: **basic** already `x≦−(465)/(76)`, `x≦(111)/(10)` — P1.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Boundary-first; basic integer boundary, advanced halves/thirds, challenge den ≤ 12.
  2. [P2] Add `strict` 「≦ 與 ＜ 混用」 fine; add `numeratorBoth` `(x−1)/(2)−(x＋2)/(3)≧1` (present) — ok.

### Unit dec 小數型（分數、小數混合）
- Variety: decimal coefficients, decimal scalars on brackets, `0.1((3x＋6)/(5))` nested forms (the last is odd — textbooks don't multiply a decimal by a fraction-with-x). 
- Difficulty: basic `x>−(352)/(5)`, `x≧(75)/(11)` — P1.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Boundary-first; drop the `0.1((3x＋6)/(5))` nested form or restrict to challenge.
  2. [P3] Keep `−0.8(0.9x＋0.5)<1.2` (two-decimal products) at advanced only.

### Unit comb 合併形式（a＜b＜c、聯立）
- Variety: `a<L<c`, `L1<L2<L3`, 聯立, with brackets and fractions by level; 「無解」 cases included. Excellent; the worked answer format (由①…由②…故…) is exactly what teachers want.
- Difficulty: good ladder; challenge fraction `x≦(114)/(155)` ugly.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Boundary-first for both sub-inequalities so the final interval has endpoints with den ≤ 6.
  2. [P2] Add `intCount` 「整數解」 to comb at advanced/challenge: `滿足 −3＜2x−1≦5 的整數有幾個？並列出` (the most common 會考 form for this unit).
  3. [P3] Decimal mode has no decimals — hide, or use decimal coefficients.

### Unit gdraw 圖示法：畫數線
- Variety: single-ended, double-ended, reversed notation `4>x`; fraction/decimal endpoints by mode. Good.
- Difficulty: challenge == basic (both integer endpoints) — flat; ladder is really by mode.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Challenge: `twoRanges` 「或」 `x＜−2 或 x≧3` (needed for abs anyway), `fromWords` `所有大於 −1 且不大於 4 的數`, and `unionInt` `畫出 −2≦x＜3 並標出其中的整數`.
  2. [P3] Keep ≤ 1 reversed-notation item per block (`4>x` reads awkwardly on a print sheet but is a legit 翰林 form).

### Unit gread 圖示法：讀數線
- Variety: read range; 「判斷 x＝k 是否在範圍內」 at challenge (good).
- Difficulty: ok.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Advanced/challenge: `readThenSolve` `如圖的範圍是不等式 2x＋a＞1 的解，求 a` (反求), `readCount` `圖中範圍內的整數有幾個？`.

### Unit sgraph 解不等式並圖示
- Variety: solve + draw; comb forms at challenge. Good.
- Difficulty: good, but inherits ugly boundaries (`x≦(137)/(77)`, `x>−(799)/(81)`) which are also **undrawable on a number line** — P1.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Boundary-first with den ≤ 4 at every level (the drawing requires it).

### Unit abs 絕對值不等式
- Variety: |ax＋b|≦k → four inequality signs, negative a → fraction/decimal coefficients, constant outside, fraction inside. Thorough and correct.
- Curriculum: **not in 108 國中** (A-7-8 covers single linear inequalities; 絕對值不等式 is 高一 數學 1). Keep as 補充 for 資優/先修, but it is in **basic presets** (`abs:2`) — a 國一 teacher printing the default basic sheet gets 高一 content.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Rename `絕對值不等式（補充，高一）`, remove from all presets, default unchecked (the `function` topic already uses this pattern).
  2. [P2] If kept: basic should be `|x|＜3`, `|x−2|≦1` with number line (currently fine); challenge fraction coefficients produce `−(58)/(27)≦x≦−(50)/(27)` — boundary-first here too.

### Unit mixed 綜合練習
- Variety: all forms. Good.
- Proposed changes: inherits boundary fix; [P3] cap `abs` share ≤ 1 per block while it is off-syllabus.

### Topic-level gaps (inequality)
- [P2] 「列不等式」 from words (`x 的 3 倍減 5 不大於 10`, `某數的一半加 3 小於 7`) — A-7-7 explicitly; cheap to add as a basic kind in `basic`.
- [P2] 應用題 (`最多可買幾枝`, `至少要考幾分`) — separate 應用 topic recommended, or 2 kinds inside `mixed`.
- [P1] Global: implement a shared `niceBoundary()` helper for all inequality units (pick x₀, then coefficients) — it fixes basic/bracket/frac/dec/comb/sgraph/abs in one place.
