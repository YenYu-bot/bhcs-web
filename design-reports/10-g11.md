# g11-drills.html design review — sequences / series / standarddev / correlation / counting / permutations / combinations / probability / righttrig / generalpolar / sincosarea / radians / sector / anglesum

Source reviewed: `/home/claude/yenyu-bot/bhcs-web/tools/math/g11-drills.html` (branch `fix/math-answer-errors`), topic configs + `presets` per topic, `UNIT_POLICIES` (line 2640–2675), `applyUnitPolicies` (line 2676). Samples from chunk `10-g11.txt`.

## Summary
- Coverage is broad (154 units across 14 topics) and mathematically clean. As teaching material, however, it falls short in three systematic ways. **(1) Level differentiation is missing** in 6 topics with flat presets (sequences, series, counting, permutations, combinations, probability). Most generators elsewhere only widen a random range, so 「挑戰」 is often the basic template with bigger numbers, and sometimes easier: 「計算 C^5_1」, 「a_n＝a_(n−1)＋1」, 「sin(π)/(12)＜sin(π)/(4)」. **(2) Many units are single-template** and miss the standard 參考書 types for the unit. Examples: 正因數個數, 塗色, 三集合容斥, 定序排列, 幾何組合, 常數項, 機率性質, 弓形面積, sin1/sin2/sin3, tanα、tanβ 為方程式兩根. **(3) Realism and formatting:** 20–50% interest rates, 7- or 9-faced dice, 48–60 °C daily temperatures, mixed numbers on 高中 pages, coefficients 「1n」 and 「−1（x−17）」, and units that print both sinθ and cosθ so the identity is never needed.
- **Curriculum (X1):** 11 of the 14 topics are 高一下 (108 第二冊), not 高二. Only radians, sector and anglesum are 高二上. Var(X＋Y) (correlation.variance) and linearity of expectation (probability.linearity) go beyond 108 必修 and should be labelled 補充.
- Highest-value fixes: X2 (real presets and level gating), X6 (mixed numbers off), righttrig complement/identities/algebra rewrite, generalpolar/anglesum quadrant cases, the 90°-heavy pools in sincosarea, the realistic interest/annuity/application contexts, and the duplicate units (counting.digits vs permutations.digits; routes vs paths; fromsums vs affine; identities vs algebra).

## Cross-cutting findings (apply to every topic in this chunk)

- **X1 [P1] Curriculum placement.** Under 108課綱 the first 11 topics in this chunk are **高一下 (必修第二冊)**, not 高二: sequences, series, standarddev, correlation (數列與級數、數據分析), counting, permutations, combinations, probability (排列組合、機率；二項式定理 also 高一), and righttrig, generalpolar, sincosarea (第二冊「三角比」: 直角三角形三角比、廣義角與極坐標、正餘弦定理). Only **radians, sector, anglesum** are 高二上 (第三冊 A/B「三角函數」). Either move the 11 topics to a g10 page / label them 「高一下（第二冊）」 in the topic selector & `<title>`, or at minimum add a grade tag in each `subtitle`. Teachers searching for 高一下 material will not look in g11-drills.
- **X2 [P1] Flat presets = no level differentiation.** `sequences`, `series`, `counting`, `permutations`, `combinations`, `probability` have presets that are *identical* across basic/advanced/challenge (every unit ×1). Combined with generators whose only level effect is a wider random range (often only `n`), the three levels of these six topics print essentially the same worksheet. The page's own `levels` text (e.g. series: 「挑戰：最大部分和、裂項相消、複利與定期定額模型」) is contradicted because basic already contains `arithmax`, `telescoping`, `annuity`. Fix: (a) give each of these topics real level presets matching the `levels` text (proposed per topic below), and (b) gate kinds by level inside generators as proposed per unit.
- **X3 [P2] "Number mode" checkboxes are really unit filters.** `UNIT_POLICIES` pins most units to a single mode (e.g. probability: only `samplespace` is integer; sequences: only `geometricterm` is fraction). Unticking 「分數」 silently drops units; ticking only 「整數」 on probability gives a one-unit sheet. Either hide the mode row for topics where every unit has one fixed mode (sequences, series, standarddev, correlation, probability, righttrig, generalpolar, anglesum, radians), or relabel it 「題型含分數答案」 and show which units each mode controls.
- **X4 [P2] "Challenge = bigger n".** In about half the units the only level hook is the upper bound of a `rand()`. A bigger index (a_1899 instead of a_655) is not harder. Proposed per unit: put reverse problems, parameters, word problems and multi-step items in the higher levels.
- **X5 [P3] Literal underscores in tips.** Several 提示 strings are plain text with `a_q`, `a_p`, `r^(q−p)` (e.g. sequences `arithmeticinfer`, `arithmeticrelation`, `geometricinfer`). Render them with `<sub>`/`<sup>` like the questions do.
- **X6 [P1] Mixed numbers on 高中 pages.** `radians`, `sector` and `anglesum` set `mixed:true`, and the 「可用帶分數」 box is checked by default. The result is items such as 「tanα＝2(1)/(3)」, 「半徑為 5(2)/(9)」, 「−652(1)/(2)（即 −(1305)/(2)）°」 and double answers 「1(58)/(59)（即 (117)/(59)）」. 高中 textbooks never use 帶分數. Set `mixed:false` (or leave the box unchecked by default) for every g11 topic, and print one answer form only.
- **X7 [P2] Arbitrary multipliers.** righttrig and generalpolar stems such as 「求 9sinθ」, 「求 23×（1−cos²θ）」 and 「7sinθ＞0，3cosθ＞0」 add noise without adding skill. Remove the multipliers unless they create a genuine combined expression.

## Topic: sequences — 數列與遞迴數列  (108: 高一下 第二冊 Ch1)
- Levels promised: 基礎「代入一般項、週期、等差與等比數列的基本公式」／進階「由已知項反求、臨界項、中項、插項與索引關係」／挑戰「數列變換、一次遞迴、循環遞迴與較大項數」.
- Overall: 15 units cover the computational core well, but every preset is ×1 at every level (X2) and 12 of 15 generators have a single form. Missing standard 高一 types: **觀察規律寫一般項**, **判斷某數是否為數列的項**, **三數成等差/等比求未知數 x**, **等差/等比判別**, **由遞迴式寫一般項**. Sample answers also showed `5n²−1n` (coefficient 1 not suppressed).
- Proposed presets: basic `{explicit:2,periodic:1,arithmeticterm:2,arithmeticindex:1,arithmeticmean:1,geometricterm:2,geometricmean:1}`; advanced `{arithmeticinfer:2,arithmeticindex:1,threshold:1,arithmeticmean:1,arithmeticrelation:1,geometricinfer:2,geometricindex:1,recurrence:1}`; challenge `{explicit:1,arithmeticrelation:1,geometricinfer:1,transform:2,recurrence:2,recurrenceperiodic:2,periodic:1}`.

### Unit explicit 由一般項求指定項
- Variety: 3 kinds (linear, quadratic, (−1)^n·k+b), all at every level; levels identical apart from range.
- Difficulty: inverted in places. Basic has 「已知 a_n＝3n²＋16n＋13，求 a_26。」 (2457, heavy arithmetic) while challenge has 「已知 a_n＝n−9，求 a_21。」.
- Plausibility: bug 「已知 a_n＝5n²−1n＋12」. The coefficient formatter does not drop the 1.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Fix the coefficient 1 → `5n²−n＋12`. Use the `coef()` helper already used in `transform`.
  2. [P1] Level-gate: basic = linear or (−1)^n with n ≤ 12; advanced = quadratic with n ≤ 15 plus the reverse form 「已知 a_n＝n²−3n＋5，則 59 是此數列的第幾項？」 (answer 第 9 項); challenge = 「寫出一般項」 and 「判斷是否為項」.
  3. [P2] New challenge kind 觀察寫一般項: 「數列 2，6，12，20，30，…，寫出一般項 a_n 並求 a_20。」 (a_n＝n（n＋1）；420). Also 「1，−4，9，−16，…」 → (−1)^(n＋1)n².
  4. [P2] New advanced kind 判斷題: 「已知 a_n＝4n＋3，問 2026 是否為此數列中的一項？若是，為第幾項？」 (否). Mix answers 是／否 about 50/50.

### Unit periodic 週期數列求項
- Variety: 1 form; the values are random integers from −20 to 30, so the cycle has no visible pattern.
- Difficulty: identical across levels (only the cycle length, up to 8, and n, up to 3000, grow).
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Challenge: ask for a sum over the cycle, e.g. 「數列 1，3，−2，1，3，−2，……，求前 100 項的和。」 (33×2＋1＝67).
  2. [P3] Use smaller, patterned cycles (e.g. 2，−1，3) at basic; keep 5–8-term cycles for advanced.

### Unit arithmeticterm 等差數列的一般項
- Variety: 1 form (given a₁ and d, find a_n); levels identical.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Advanced: give the first three terms instead of a₁ and d: 「等差數列 7，3，−1，…，求第 30 項。」
  2. [P2] Challenge: 三數成等差求 x: 「若 x−1，2x＋1，5x−3 成等差數列，求 x 及公差。」 (2(2x＋1)＝6x−4 → x＝3, d＝5). Also the general-term form 「求一般項 a_n」 (answer like a_n＝4n−1).

### Unit arithmeticinfer 由兩項反求首項與公差
- Variety: 2 kinds (find params / find a_t); basic is restricted to params. The ladder is OK.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P3] Challenge: a sum/product condition: 「等差數列中 a_3＋a_7＝26，a_4・a_6＝160，求公差。」 (a_5＝13, (13−d)(13＋d)＝160 → d＝±3).

### Unit arithmeticindex 等差數列反求項數
- Variety: 1 form; levels identical.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Advanced/challenge: count terms in a range: 「等差數列 5，12，19，…中，介於 100 與 500 之間的項共有幾項？」 (a_n＝7n−2 → n＝15…71 → 57 項).
  2. [P2] Challenge: non-member check: 「首項 3、公差 4 的等差數列中，2027 是否為其中一項？」 (Yes, term 507. Make about half of the items 否.)

### Unit threshold 等差數列的正負臨界項
- Variety: 2 kinds (first term below 0 / first term above 0). Fine, but the levels are identical.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P3] Challenge: use a fractional d or a non-zero threshold: 「首項 50、公差 −(3)/(4)，從第幾項起小於 −10？」.

### Unit arithmeticmean 等差中項與插項
- Variety: 1 form (insert k numbers). The tip says 等差中項, but no item asks for a 等差中項.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Basic: add the 等差中項 kind 「求 −8 與 20 的等差中項。」 (6).
  2. [P3] Challenge: insertion with a non-integer d: 「在 1 與 4 之間插入 5 個數成等差，求第 3 個插入的數。」 (d＝1/2 → 5/2). This unit is integer-only, so it needs MF.

### Unit arithmeticrelation 等差數列的對稱項
- Variety: 1 form at all levels, 「等差數列滿足 a_22＋a_37＝a_29＋a_k，求 k。」 This is index arithmetic only (k＝22＋37−29) and never uses term values.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Replace it with the standard value form: 「等差數列中，a_3＋a_11＝40，求 a_7。」 (20) at basic/advanced, and 「a_2＋a_5＋a_8＋a_11＝60，求 a_1＋a_12。」 (30) at challenge. Keep the k-form as at most 1 item at basic.

### Unit geometricterm 等比數列的一般項
- Variety: 1 form. It is pinned to fraction mode (X3), so an integer-only sheet has no 等比一般項 at all.
- Difficulty: no gating except n ≤ 12. Advanced samples (「首項 9，公比 3，求 a_4」) are easier than basic samples.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Make it `["integer","fraction"]`: integer mode uses r＝±2, ±3; fraction mode uses r＝±1/2, ±2/3.
  2. [P2] Advanced: give three terms 「等比數列 48，−24，12，…，求第 8 項。」. Challenge: 三數成等比求 x 「x，x＋2，x＋8 成等比，求 x。」 ((x＋2)²＝x(x＋8) → x＝1).

### Unit geometricinfer 由已知項求等比數列
- Variety: 1 form.
- Plausibility: 「a_4＝−64，a_7＝512，且公比與 −2 同號」. The gap is always 1 or 3 (odd), so r is already unique and the clause adds nothing; it is unnatural and gives the answer away. Textbooks use an even gap plus 「公比為正（負）」.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Use gap ∈ {2, 4} with 「且公比為負」 (or 正), e.g. 「等比數列中 a_2＝12，a_4＝108，且公比為負，求 a_6。」 (r＝−3 → 972). Keep the odd-gap version without the clause.
  2. [P2] Challenge: 「等比數列中 a_2＋a_3＝12，a_3＋a_4＝36，求 a_1。」 (r＝3, a_1＝1).

### Unit geometricindex 等比數列反求項數
- Good; no structural change. [P3] Challenge: allow r＝1/2, 「首項 96、公比 (1)/(2)，(3)/(8) 是第幾項？」 (第 9 項).

### Unit geometricmean 等比中項與插項
- Variety: 1 form; positive ratio only. The tip mentions 中項平方, but no item asks for a 中項.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Basic: 「求 4 與 9 的等比中項。」 (±6; point out to students that there are two answers).
  2. [P3] Challenge: real insertion with two answers: 「在 3 與 48 之間插入 3 個實數使成等比，求插入的數。」 (6，12，24 或 −6，12，−24).

### Unit transform 數列的線性變換
- Variety: 1 form; levels identical.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Challenge: geometric/exponential transforms: 「〈a_n〉為等差數列，公差 2。令 b_n＝3^(a_n)，問〈b_n〉是否為等比數列？公比為何？」 (是，9). Also 「〈a_n〉、〈b_n〉皆等差，c_n＝a_n＋2b_n 的公差」.

### Unit recurrence 一次遞迴逐項計算
- Variety: 1 form (a_n＝p·a_(n−1)＋q).
- Difficulty: inverted. Challenge has 「a₁＝11，a_n＝a_(n−1)＋1，求 a_7。」 while basic has 「a₁＝10，a_n＝3a_(n−1)＋6，求 a_6。」 (3156).
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Basic: p＝1 (等差型) or q＝0 (等比型), n ≤ 6, |values| < 200. Advanced: p＝±2, ±3, n ≤ 6. Challenge: new forms.
  2. [P2] Challenge new kinds: 階差型 「a₁＝1，a_(n＋1)＝a_n＋2n，求 a_10。」 (91); 二階 「a₁＝1，a₂＝1，a_(n＋2)＝a_(n＋1)＋a_n，求 a_10。」 (55); 由遞迴寫一般項 「a₁＝5，a_(n＋1)＝a_n−3，寫出 a_n。」 (a_n＝8−3n).

### Unit recurrenceperiodic 遞迴數列的循環
- Variety: 1 form (a_n＝c−a_(n−1), a 2-cycle).
- Plausibility: when c＝0 it prints 「a_n＝0−a_n−1」.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] When c＝0, print 「a_n＝−a_(n−1)」.
  2. [P2] Advanced/challenge: 3-cycle 「a₁＝2，a_(n＋1)＝(1)/(1−a_n)，求 a_100。」 (cycle 2, −1, 1/2 → a_100＝2; needs fraction mode). 6-cycle 「a₁＝1，a₂＝3，a_(n＋2)＝a_(n＋1)−a_n，求 a_2026。」 (a_2026＝a_4＝−1).

## Topic: series — 級數與常用公式  (108: 高一下 第二冊 Ch1)
- Levels promised: 基礎「等差、等比級數與首末項求和」／進階「由總和反求項數、部分和、Σ 與常用求和公式」／挑戰「最大部分和、裂項相消、複利與定期定額模型」.
- Overall: 15 units with good topical coverage, but every preset is ×1 at every level (X2), and 9 of 15 units have exactly one form with no level gating. The interest/annuity rates (20–50%) are unrealistic. `fromsum` never asks for a₁ (the key teaching point). Missing standard types: **以 Σ 表示級數**, **某範圍內倍數和**, **S_n 兩值求第三值**, **Σ k(k＋1) 型**, **裂項的其他型**, **S_n 不等式求最小 n**.
- Proposed presets: basic `{arithsum:2,arithends:2,arithlast:2,geosum:2,geolast:1,oddeven:1}`; advanced `{arithinverse:2,fromsum:2,sigma:2,powersums:2,geoinverse:1,interest:1}`; challenge `{arithmax:2,telescoping:2,fromsum:1,powersums:1,interest:2,annuity:2}`.

### Unit arithsum 等差級數求和
- Variety: 1 form; levels identical (n up to 80).
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Advanced: 倍數和 「求 1 到 200 之間所有 7 的倍數之和。」 (7＋14＋…＋196＝28 項 → 2842).
  2. [P2] Challenge: 「等差數列前 10 項和為 100，前 20 項和為 400，求前 30 項和。」 (900).

### Unit arithends 已知首末項求和
- Good; it overlaps with arithlast. [P3] Basic: cap n ≤ 30. Advanced: phrase it as a real situation 「劇場第一排 20 個座位，之後每排多 2 個，共 25 排，最後一排幾個？全場共幾個座位？」 (68；1100).

### Unit arithlast 由末項求項數與總和
- Good; no change.

### Unit arithinverse 由部分和反求項數
- Variety: 1 form at all levels.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Advanced: a negative d that gives two valid n: 「首項 20、公差 −2 的等差數列，前 n 項和為 90，求 n。」 (n²−21n＋90＝0 → n＝6 或 15). Students must keep both and explain why.
  2. [P2] Challenge: inequality: 「首項 3、公差 4，前 n 項和首次超過 1000 時 n 至少為多少？」 (2n²＋n＞1000 → n＝23).

### Unit arithmax 遞減等差級數的最大部分和
- Variety: 1 form; the levels are identical, yet the page puts this unit in 挑戰.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Challenge: the zero-term tie case 「首項 24、公差 −3，前幾項和最大？」 (a₉＝0 → S₈＝S₉＝108, two answers). Also 「a₁＞0 且 S₁₀＝S₁₈，問前幾項和最大？」 (前 14 項).

### Unit geosum 等比級數求和
- Fraction-only (X3); there is no integer version. The ladder is mild.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Make it `["integer","fraction"]`.
  2. [P2] Challenge: 「等比級數前 3 項和為 7，前 6 項和為 63，求公比與首項。」 (r³＝8 → r＝2, a₁＝1).

### Unit geolast 由末項求等比級數
- Plausibility: 「等比級數 1＋2＋4＋…＋8」 uses an ellipsis for a 4-term series, so nothing is hidden.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Require at least 6 terms when 「…」 is printed.
  2. [P2] Advanced/challenge: allow r＝−2 and r＝1/2: 「等比級數 64−32＋16−…＋1，求項數與總和。」 (7 項；43).

### Unit geoinverse 由總和反求等比項數
- Fine; the challenge item (S_n＝52) is easier than basic. [P3] Give challenge r＝2 with a larger n, or the inequality form 「前 n 項和首次超過 1000」.

### Unit fromsum 由部分和求數列項
- Variety: 1 form (ask for a_n with n ≥ 2).
- Plausibility: S_n always has a constant term (e.g. 「S_n＝9n²＋3n−17」), so a₁＝S₁ breaks the a_n formula. That is the whole teaching point of the unit, and a₁ is never asked. The answer formatter also prints 「4n²−1n」 and 「2n²＋1n」.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Fix the coefficient 1.
  2. [P1] Add the kind 「S_n＝2n²＋3n＋1，求 a₁ 與 a_n（n≥2），並判斷〈a_n〉是否為等差數列。」 (a₁＝6；a_n＝4n＋1；否). Put it in advanced and challenge, and keep the plain single-term form for basic with a zero constant term.
  3. [P3] Challenge: 「S_n＝3·2ⁿ−3，求 a_n」 (等比 a_n＝3·2^(n−1)).

### Unit sigma Σ 線性求和
- Variety: linear Σ(ak＋b) only, from k＝1. The levels are identical.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Basic: notation kinds 「將 2＋5＋8＋…＋59 以 Σ 表示。」 (∑_(k＝1)^20 (3k−1)) and 「寫出 ∑_(k＝1)^4 k² 的展開式」.
  2. [P2] Advanced: a lower limit other than 1, 「計算 ∑_(k＝11)^30（2k＋1）。」 (2×410＋20＝840). Challenge: quadratic summands 「計算 ∑_(k＝1)^10 k（k＋1）。」 (440).

### Unit powersums 自然數平方和與立方和
- Variety: plain Σk², Σk³ with a constant multiple.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Advanced: 「11²＋12²＋…＋20²」 (2485). Challenge: 「1×2＋2×3＋…＋20×21」 (3080) and 「1²＋3²＋5²＋…＋19²」 (1330).

### Unit oddeven 奇數和與偶數和
- Variety: 2 near-trivial forms, the same at all levels. It duplicates arithsum.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Keep it at basic only. Replace 「求前 n 個正奇數的總和」 with the range form 「求 21＋23＋25＋…＋99 的值。」 (2500−100＝2400), so the n² trick has to be applied rather than recited.

### Unit telescoping 裂項相消級數
- Variety: 1 form (c／〔k（k＋1）〕) at all levels.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Advanced: 「∑_(k＝1)^10 1／〔（2k−1）（2k＋1）〕」 (10/21).
  2. [P2] Challenge: 「∑_(k＝1)^20 1／〔k（k＋2）〕」 (1/2（1＋1/2−1/21−1/22）＝325/462) and the root form 「∑_(k＝1)^24 1／（√k＋√（k＋1））」 (√25−1＝4).

### Unit interest 單利與複利
- Plausibility: 「本金 2256 元，年利率 50%，存 2 年」 and 年利率 25%. These rates are unrealistic and the principals are artificial.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Use realistic rates (1%–5%), principals such as 10 萬, and supply the power value: 「本金 100000 元，年利率 2%，按年複利存 3 年，到期本利和約多少元？（1.02³＝1.061208）」 (106121 元). Allow 「四捨五入至整數元」 answers (decimal mode).
  2. [P2] Challenge: 月複利 「年利率 1.2%，每月複利一次，存 2 年」 (rate 0.1% per month, 24 periods, 1.001²⁴ given). Also compare simple vs compound interest.

### Unit annuity 定期定額存款
- Plausibility: the same unrealistic 20–25% rates as interest; only 3–4 deposits.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Use realistic rates with supplied powers: 「每年年底存入 30000 元，年利率 2%，按年複利，第 5 次存款後本利和約多少元？（1.02⁵≈1.10408）」 (30000×(1.10408−1)/0.02＝156120 元).
  2. [P3] Challenge: the reverse form 「希望 5 年後存到 20 萬，每年年底需存多少元？」.

## Topic: standarddev — 標準差與標準化數據  (108: 高一下 第二冊「一維數據分析」)

- Good (same mean, judge by eye). [P3] Challenge: add a 判斷題 such as 「每筆資料都加 5，標準差會變大、變小或不變？」 (不變) and 「每筆乘 −2」 (變為 2 倍).

### Unit rawstats 由原始資料求平均數與標準差
- Variety: 1 form (5 raw numbers).

### Unit moments 由總和與平方和求標準差
- Good. [P3] Vary n (always 5 now) and use a 10-student context: 「10 位同學身高總和 1650 cm、平方和 272750，求標準差。」 (μ＝165, σ²＝27275−27225＝50 → 5√2).

### Unit deviationsum 離均差平方和
- Fine; it is mixed into advanced/challenge as a reverse form. Good; no change.

### Unit transform 線性調整後的統計量
- Variety: 1 abstract form (y＝ax＋b); no context.

  1. [P2] Contexts at basic/advanced: 「老師將全班分數乘 0.8 再加 20 分，原平均 55、標準差 15，調整後平均與標準差？」 (64，12). 「攝氏平均 25°C、標準差 5°C，換成華氏 F＝1.8C＋32？」 (77°F，9°F). Also ask for the variance (σ² scales by a²).

### Unit reversetransform 反推原資料的統計量
- Fine. [P2] Challenge: the parameter-reverse form 「調整 y＝ax＋b（a＞0）使平均由 50 變 70、標準差由 10 變 8，求 a、b。」 (a＝0.8，b＝30). This is a classic 學測 type.

### Unit zscore 計算標準分數
- Variety: 1 form, always 「小明」; levels identical.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Rotate names and contexts (身高、跑步秒數). Challenge: 「跑步時間越短越好」 so the z sign has to be interpreted.

### Unit zcompare 用標準分數比較表現
- Good (3 kinds plus a 3-subject ranking at challenge); no change.

### Unit zproperties 標準化資料的性質
- Variety: 1 form at all levels, 「求 Σzᵢ 與 Σzᵢ²」, recitation only.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Keep it at advanced. Challenge: 「y＝−2x＋5，某筆資料 x 的標準分數為 1.5，則對應 y 的標準分數為何？」 (−1.5). Also 「資料標準化後，再乘 10 加 50，新平均與標準差？」 (50，10; this is how T 分數 works).

### Unit zinverse 由標準分數反求原分數
- Good (challenge reverses to find μ). [P3] Challenge: find σ and μ from two students: 「甲 80 分 z＝1，乙 60 分 z＝−1.5，求平均與標準差。」 (σ＝8，μ＝72).

### Unit standardlist 整組資料標準化
- Good; no change.

### Unit correctmean 更正資料後的平均數
- Plausibility: answers such as 「(461)/(12)」 for a mean. Textbooks pick numbers that give integer or one-decimal answers. Single form.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Choose n whose only prime factors are 2 and 5 (or an error difference divisible by n) so the answer is an integer or a terminating decimal: 「40 位同學平均 62 分，後來發現一位 85 分誤登為 58 分，求正確平均。」 (62＋27/40＝62.675). Allow decimal mode.

### Unit correctvariance 更正資料後的變異數
- Plausibility: n is always 5 and the variance is often the odd 「(234)/(5)」. Answers such as 「(5064)/(25)」.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Use n＝10–20 with a nice μ and σ: 「10 筆資料平均 50、標準差 5，發現其中 45 誤記為 55，求正確平均數與標準差。」 (μ＝49；Σx²＝10·2525＝25250 → −3025＋2025＝24250 → σ²＝2425−2401＝24 → 2√6).

### Unit groupmean 合併兩組資料的平均數

- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Choose sizes that give integer or one-decimal means (e.g. 20 and 30 students). Advanced: the reverse form 「男生 20 人平均 70，全班 50 人平均 74，求女生平均。」 → use 全班 50 人平均 76 (女生 80).

### Unit groupvariance 合併兩組資料的變異數
- Variety: equal group sizes only.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Challenge: unequal sizes: 「甲班 20 人平均 70、標準差 10；乙班 30 人平均 80、標準差 5，求兩班合併標準差。」 (μ＝76；Σx²＝20·5000＋30·6425＝292750 → σ²＝5855−5776＝79 → √79).

## Topic: correlation — 相關係數與迴歸直線  (108: 高一下 第二冊「二維數據分析」)
- Levels promised: 基礎「散布圖、相關方向、完全線性相關與迴歸線直接代入」／進階「由資料或總和求 r、線性變換、迴歸斜率與預測」／挑戰「和的變異數、反向迴歸、雙斜率、變數變換與情境模型」.
- Overall: the presets follow the level text and the unit set is rich. Gaps:
  - The most standard textbook item, **由成對資料求迴歸直線**, is missing. `line` only works from summary statistics.
  - There are no **conceptual 判斷題** (r＝0 ≠ 無關、相關 ≠ 因果、外推).
  - `application` has a single, unrealistic context.
  - Several answers print coefficients 1, −1 or 「（x）」.
  - `fromsums` duplicates `affine`.
  - `variance` (Var(X＋Y) with r) is beyond 108 必修. Mark it 「補充」 or drop it from the challenge preset.

### Unit scatter 由散布圖判斷相關方向
- Variety: 1 form (direction only).
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Advanced: strength matching 「下列四個散布圖的相關係數分別為 0.9、0.3、−0.5、−0.95，請配對。」 Challenge: an outlier effect item 「去掉離群點後 r 會變大或變小？」.

### Unit rcompare 比較相關性的強弱
- Variety: 1 form, the same at all levels.
- Proposed changes:

### Unit fromdata 由成對資料計算相關係數
- Good (equally spaced x makes hand computation feasible). [P3] Challenge: use 6 points or unequal spacing.

### Unit fromsums 由五個總和計算相關係數
- Plausibility: half of the items are affine-transform r questions (「r＝0.2。令 u＝3x＋6，求 u 與 y 的相關係數。」), which duplicates `affine`. The tip also bundles three ideas.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Remove the affine kinds and keep the Σ kinds. Shorten the tip to 「r＝（nΣxy−ΣxΣy）／√〔（nΣx²−（Σx）²）（nΣy²−（Σy）²）〕；已知 r 可反求 Σxy。」.

### Unit affine 線性變換後的相關係數
- Good; no change.

### Unit perfect 完全線性相關
- Basic only (OK). [P3] Add the data form 「資料 （1，4）、（3，10）、（5，16）、（7，22） 的相關係數為何？」 (1), so students recognise collinear points without being told the equation.

### Unit variance 相關係數與和的變異數
- Curriculum: Var(X＋Y)＝σₓ²＋σᵧ²＋2rσₓσᵧ is not in 108 必修 (共變異數 is not taught in 高一). It appears only in 補充/參考書.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Label it 「（補充）」 in the unit name, or move it out of the challenge preset into an optional unit.

### Unit slope 由相關係數求迴歸斜率
- Good; no change.

### Unit line 求迴歸直線方程式
- Plausibility: answers 「y−11＝(9)/(20)（x）」 and 「y−25＝−1（x−17）」. The point-slope form is acceptable, but textbooks usually expect y＝mx＋b.
- Proposed changes:

### Unit inferr 由迴歸斜率反求相關係數
- Good. [P3] Add an impossibility 判斷題 to twoslopes (see below).

### Unit predict 利用迴歸直線預測

### Unit reverse 反向迴歸斜率
- Good; no change.

### Unit twoslopes 由雙向迴歸斜率求相關係數
- Good. [P2] Challenge: add 「Y 對 X 斜率為 2、X 對 Y 斜率為 (2)/(3)，是否可能？」 (r²＝4/3＞1，不可能).

### Unit multiplication 乘法原理

  1. [P1] Require r ≥ 2.
  2. [P2] Vary contexts beyond 候選人 (書排上書架、旗子排信號、比賽冠亞季軍): 「10 人參加比賽，取前三名（冠亞季軍）的情形有幾種？」 (720).

### Unit fixed 指定位置排列

## Topic: righttrig — 直角三角形的三角比  (108: 高一下 第二冊「三角比」)
- Levels promised: 基礎「三邊定義、30°／45°／60°特殊角與單一步驟求邊」／進階「三角比互換、餘角、平方關係、斜邊高與仰角測量」／挑戰「多步驟代數化簡、雙觀測點、方位測距與大小比較」.

That turns 「平方關係」 and 「餘角」 into plain substitution: 「sinθ＝(4)/(5)、cosθ＝(3)/(5)。求 23×（1−cos²θ）」. They never test the identity, and the arbitrary multipliers (23×, 29×) are unnatural. ### Unit definitions 由三邊求三角比
- Variety: 1 form. All three sides are given and always form a Pythagorean triple; challenge is just scaled (「對邊＝152、鄰邊＝114、斜邊＝190」).
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Give two sides only and include non-triples: 「直角三角形兩股為 2、3，求較小銳角 θ 的 sinθ、cosθ、tanθ。」 (斜邊 √13 → sinθ＝(2√13)/(13)…). Rationalised answers go to advanced.
  2. [P2] Add an SVG right triangle with labelled sides at basic, and ask for the ratios of the other acute angle as well.

### Unit fromone 由一個三角比反求
- Plausibility: 「已知 cosθ＝(4)/(5)，求 9sinθ」. The multiplier 9 is arbitrary. Only 3-4-5 and 5-12-13 appear. The answer 「(1267)/(65)」 is ugly.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Ask 「求 sinθ 與 tanθ」 (no multipliers). Advanced: non-triple values 「已知 sinθ＝(1)/(3)，求 cosθ、tanθ。」 (cosθ＝(2√2)/(3)，tanθ＝(√2)/(4)). Challenge: 「tanθ＝2，求 (sinθ＋cosθ)/(sinθ−cosθ)」 (3, divide by cosθ).

### Unit special 30°、45°、60°特殊角計算

### Unit complement 餘角關係
- Plausibility: 「sinθ＝(4)/(5)、cosθ＝(3)/(5)。求 8cos（90°−θ）」 (substitution only).
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Replace it with standard 餘角 items: 「求 sin²20°＋sin²70° 的值。」 (1); 「求 tan10°×tan20°×…×tan80° 的值。」 (1); 「sin（90°−θ）＝(3)/(5)，θ 為銳角，求 tanθ」 (4/3); 「若 sinA＝cos B 且 A、B 為銳角，求 A＋B」 (90°).

### Unit identities 平方關係與代數化簡
- Plausibility: the same problem, e.g. 「sinθ＝(3)/(5)、cosθ＝(4)/(5)。求 11×（1＋tan²θ）」. It also duplicates `algebra`.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Advanced 化簡: 「化簡（1−sin²θ）（1＋tan²θ）。」 (1); 「化簡 sinθ／（1＋cosθ）＋（1＋cosθ）／sinθ」 (2／sinθ); 「已知 sinθ＝(5)/(13)，求 1＋tan²θ」 (169/144). Drop the stems that give both sinθ and cosθ.

  1. [P2] Advanced: the 射影定理 reverse 「直角△ABC 斜邊上的高 CD 把 AB 分成 AD＝4、DB＝9，求 CD、AC。」 (6；2√13). Challenge: 「兩股 6、8，求斜邊上的高與兩段射影」, with only the legs given.

### Unit elevation 單點仰角與俯角

- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Add 俯角: 「從高 30 公尺的大樓頂樓看地面上一輛車，俯角為 30°，求車到大樓底部的距離。」 (30√3 公尺).

- Levels promised: 基礎「同界角、象限、特殊角、終邊坐標與基本極座標互換」／進階「指定區間、三角比關係、直線斜角、關係角與方位定位」／挑戰「多步驟三角式、象限條件、兩直線夾角與綜合座標轉換」.
- Overall: strong unit set with sensible presets; the [r，θ] notation and 「南30°西」 bearings match Taiwanese textbooks. Weak points:
  - Arbitrary multipliers again (「7sinθ＞0，3cosθ＞0」, 「求 9cos（180°−θ）」).
  - Only 3-4-5 and 5-12-13 values appear.
  - `slope` mostly produces 斜率 1/0/−1.
  - Missing standard types: **θ 在某象限時 θ/2、2θ、−θ 的象限**, **終邊在直線上**, **關係角的式子化簡**, **由直角坐標求方位**.

### Unit coterminal 最小非負同界角
- Good. [P3] Challenge: 「寫出與 −1000° 同界的角的一般式及最小正角」 (−1000°＋360°k；80°).

### Unit coterminalset 指定區間的同界角
- Plausibility: interval bounds such as 「−2370°≤θ≤−1236°」 are arbitrary. [P3] Use round bounds (−720°≤θ≤720°, 0°≤θ＜1080°) and ask 「共有幾個」 at challenge.

### Unit quadrant 象限角與坐標軸
- Variety: 1 form.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Challenge: 「θ 為第二象限角，則 θ／2 的終邊可能在第幾象限？2θ 呢？」 (θ/2：一或三；2θ：三、四或 y 軸負向). Advanced: 「−θ、180°＋θ 在第幾象限」.

### Unit signs 三角比正負判斷象限
- Plausibility: 「7sinθ＞0，3cosθ＞0」. The positive coefficients are noise.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Drop the coefficients at basic/advanced. Keep the challenge products/sums (good). Add 「sinθ・tanθ＜0」 kinds.

### Unit special 廣義角特殊值
- Good; no change.

### Unit terminalpoint 終邊上一點求三角比
- Variety: only scaled 3-4-5 and 5-12-13 points (「P（−84，112）」).
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Advanced: non-triple points 「P（−1，2）」 (r＝√5 → sinθ＝(2√5)/(5)…). Challenge: 「θ 的終邊在直線 y＝−2x 上且位於第四象限，求 sinθ、cosθ」 ((−2√5)/(5)，(√5)/(5)). Also 「P（a，−3）在終邊上且 cosθ＝(4)/(5)，求 a」 (4).

### Unit fromratio 已知三角比與象限求值
- Good structure (quadrant plus a derived expression). [P2] Add non-triple values at advanced/challenge: 「θ 為第三象限角，sinθ＝−(1)/(3)，求 cosθ、tanθ」 (−(2√2)/(3)，(√2)/(4)).

### Unit related 關係角化簡求值
- Plausibility: 「求 3sin（90°−θ）」 and 「求 12sin（−θ）」, multipliers again. Only 90°−θ, 180°−θ, 270°−θ, 360°−θ and −θ appear, one at a time.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Remove the multipliers; add 180°＋θ, 90°＋θ, 270°＋θ.
  2. [P2] Challenge: the textbook 化簡 form 「化簡 sin（180°−θ）cos（270°＋θ）＋cos（180°＋θ）sin（90°−θ）。」 (sin²θ−cos²θ). Also 「以銳角三角比表示 cos1000°」 (cos80°).

### Unit expression 廣義角三角式計算
- Good ladder (2, 3, then 4 terms). Good; no change.

### Unit slope 直線斜角與斜率
- Variety: from-two-points items almost always give 斜率 1, −1 or 0 (3 of 4 basic samples were 「斜率 1；θ＝45°」).
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Two-point items with slope ±√3 or ±(√3)/(3): 「A（1，2）、B（4，2＋√3）」 → (√3)/(3), 30°. Add 「直線 √3x−y＋1＝0 的斜角」 (60°). Basic: 「斜角 135°」 cases. Make sure each slope value appears in at most 2 of 8 items.

### Unit lineangle 直線方程式與銳夾角
- Good (the acute angle via difference of inclinations gives 30°, 45°, 60°, 75°). [P3] Output the line equation in the textbook form 「y＝x＋15」 or 「x−y＋15＝0」 consistently. Currently general form only, which is fine.

### Unit polar2cart 極座標轉直角坐標
- Variety: 1 form; basic sample 「[17，0°]」 is trivial.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P3] Basic: avoid θ＝0°, 90°, 180°, 270° in more than 1 of 8 items. Challenge: a negative or large angle 「[6，−150°]」 and 「[4，480°]」.

### Unit cart2polar 直角坐標轉極座標
- Good. [P3] Challenge: 「兩點極座標 [4，30°]、[4，150°]，求兩點距離」 (4√3).

### Unit polarapplication 方位與雷達定位
- Good contexts. Challenge is not harder than basic.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Challenge reverse: 「目標直角坐標 （−5，5√3） 公里，以方位角表示其位置與距離。」 (北30°西 10 公里). Also a two-target displacement 「A 在東北 10 公里、B 在正東 10√2 公里，求 B 相對 A 的方位」 (A＝（5√2，5√2）, B−A＝（5√2，−5√2） → 東南 10 公里).

## Topic: sincosarea — 正餘弦定理與三角形面積  (108: 高一下 第二冊「三角比」正弦定理、餘弦定理)
- Levels promised: 基礎「三角形判定、特殊角與正餘弦定理的直接代入」／進階「反求邊角、外接圓、海龍公式、中線與角平分線」／挑戰「SSA 多解判斷、多公式串聯與測距、土地面積等應用」.
- Overall: the presets match the levels, but **90° dominates the angle pools**. Examples: `sineside` basic pool (3 of 4 entries contain 90°), `cosineside` easy pool (3 of 6 are 90°, so the law reduces to 畢氏定理), `includedarea` (90° in the basic pool). Samples: 「b＝64、c＝120、∠A＝90°，求 a」 and 「b＝8、c＝3、∠A＝90°，求面積」. Every unit except ambiguous/cosineangle/application is a single template. Missing standard types:
  - **a：b：c＝sinA：sinB：sinC**
  - **已知面積反求夾角**
  - **內切圓半徑 r＝Δ／s 與 K＝abc／4R**
  - **鈍角三角形的邊長範圍**
  - **測量河寬（兩角夾一邊）**
  - **四邊形土地分割**

### Unit classification 三邊判定三角形種類
- Variety: 1 form; challenge is scaled (「90、100、120」).
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Advanced: include 「不能構成三角形」 cases (e.g. 3，5，9). Challenge parameter form: 「三角形三邊為 4、5、x，若為鈍角三角形，求 x 的範圍。」 (1＜x＜3 或 √41＜x＜9).

### Unit sineside 正弦定理求邊
- Difficulty: the basic pool is mostly right triangles; advanced and challenge share one pool, e.g. 「∠A＝90°、∠B＝45°、a＝24，求 b」 at challenge.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Use at most 1 in 4 items with a 90° angle. Advanced: give ∠B and ∠C (find ∠A first): 「∠B＝45°、∠C＝105°、a＝10，求 b。」 (10√2). Challenge: the ratio form 「△ABC 中 ∠A：∠B：∠C＝1：2：3，求 a：b：c。」 (1：√3：2) and 「sinA：sinB：sinC＝3：5：7，求最大角」 (120°).

### Unit ambiguous SSA 的三角形個數
- Variety: 3 phrasings of one task. Most samples are 唯一解 because a≥b or a＝b sinA; 兩解 was rare (1 of 14 samples) and its answer does not list the angles.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Balance the cases (無解／一解（直角）／一解（a≥b）／兩解) at roughly 25% each. For 兩解 always list both angles: 「∠A＝30°、a＝√2、b＝2，求 ∠B。」 (45° 或 135°).
  2. [P3] Challenge parameter form 「∠A＝30°、b＝10，若恰有兩個三角形，求 a 的範圍。」 (5＜a＜10).

### Unit circumradius 正弦定理與外接圓
- Variety: 1 form (a and ∠A → R); basic uses 90°.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Advanced: reverse 「外接圓半徑 R＝6，∠A＝60°，求 a」 (6√3). Challenge: 「三邊 13、14、15，求外接圓半徑」 (Heron 84 → R＝abc/4K＝65/8).

### Unit cosineside 餘弦定理求邊
- Difficulty: 「∠A＝90°」 items are 畢氏定理, not 餘弦定理 (3 of the 6 easy entries).
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Drop 90° from the pools; basic uses 60° and 120° with small numbers (5，8，60° → 7). Challenge: two sides plus a non-included angle gives a quadratic 「b＝7、c＝8、∠B＝60°…」 → 「a＝3 或 5」.

### Unit cosineangle 餘弦定理求角
- Good (3 kinds, including 120°). Good; no change.

### Unit includedarea 兩邊夾角求面積
- Difficulty: basic and advanced samples were 90° (「b＝11、c＝6、∠A＝90°」).
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Remove 90° from the basic pool (use 30°, 150°). Advanced reverse: 「△ABC 面積為 15，b＝6、c＝10，求 ∠A。」 (30° 或 150°). Challenge: parallelogram 「平行四邊形兩鄰邊 6、8，夾角 120°，求面積」 (24√3).

### Unit heron 海龍公式求面積
- Good. [P2] Challenge: chain 「並求內切圓半徑 r 與最長邊上的高」 (for 13，14，15: K＝84，r＝4，h＝84·2/15＝56/5).

### Unit median 中線長
- Curriculum: the 中線定理 (Apollonius) is 參考書/補充 level in 108. Keep it advanced, but answers such as 「(3√601)/(2)」 are heavy.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P3] Choose triangles with a nicer mₐ (e.g. b＝c＝10, a＝12 → 8). Add the reverse 「三邊 a、b 與中線 mₐ 已知求 c」.

### Unit bisector 內角平分線長
- Plausibility: the tip's formula 「bc[1−a²／(b＋c)²]」 is not taught in 108 textbooks. The textbook method is 分角線比例 BD：DC＝c：b plus area/餘弦定理. Answers such as 「(1680√5)/(29)」.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Basic/advanced: ask for BD and DC first: 「AB＝6、AC＝4、BC＝5，∠A 的平分線交 BC 於 D，求 BD。」 (3). Challenge: the area method with ∠A＝120°: 「AB＝3、AC＝6、∠A＝120°，求 AD」 ((1/2)·3·6·sin120°＝(1/2)·AD·(3＋6)·sin60° → AD＝2). Change the tip to the area-split method.

### Unit application 測距與面積應用
- Variety: 2–3 templates; challenge includes a 90° item (「6 公里與 8 公里，夾角 90°」 → 10).
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Remove 90° from challenge.
  2. [P2] Add 河寬 (正弦定理): 「河岸 A、B 相距 100 公尺，對岸 C 點測得 ∠CAB＝75°、∠CBA＝45°，求 AC。」 (∠C＝60° → AC＝100·sin45°/sin60°＝(100√6)/(3)). Add 四邊形土地: 「四邊形 ABCD 中 AB＝3、BC＝5、∠B＝120°、CD＝AD＝7，求面積。」 (AC＝7 → △ACD equilateral: (15√3)/(4)＋(49√3)/(4)＝16√3).

## Topic: radians — 弧度量  (108: 高二上 第三冊 A/B「三角函數」; correctly on g11)
- Levels promised: 基礎「常見整數角與特殊弧度互換」／進階「分數／小數角、同界角與象限」／挑戰「大角、負角、三角比比較與綜合求值」.

- Plausibility: fraction-mode answers such as 「將 −(29π)/(8) 化為角度」 → 「−652(1)/(2)（即 −(1305)/(2)）°」.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Print non-integer degrees as decimals (−652.5°) or as 度分 (−652°30′). Turn the mixed-number option off for this page (高中 does not use 帶分數). Limit fraction-mode denominators to 8, 12, 24 or 36 only when the result is a clean degree count.
  2. [P2] Advanced: radians without π: 「將 2 弧度化為角度（取 π≈3.14，四捨五入至小數第一位）」 (114.6°), and 「1 弧度約為幾度？」.

### Unit quadrant 象限與同界角
- Good (it already uses θ＝8 弧度 etc.). Good; no change.

### Unit compare 三角函數值比較
- Difficulty: inverted. Challenge 「由小到大排列：sin(π)/(4)、sin(π)/(12)、sin(π)/(3)」 (first quadrant, monotone) is easier than the basic items.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Challenge: the classic radian comparison 「比較 sin1、sin2、sin3 的大小。」 (sin3＜sin1＜sin2), and 「cos1、cos2、cos3」 (cos3＜cos2＜cos1), 「tan1、tan2、tan3」 (tan2＜tan3＜tan1). Keep special-angle items for basic/advanced.

### Unit special 特殊弧度角三角值
- Fine. [P3] Challenge: drop the trivial 「cos（(π)/(4)）」 and use expressions 「sin（(7π)/(6)）＋cos（−(2π)/(3)）tan（(5π)/(4)）」 (−1).

## Topic: anglesum — 和差角公式  (108: 高二上 第三冊 A/B「三角函數」; correctly on g11)
- Levels promised: 基礎「15°、75°等特殊角與直接公式化簡」／進階「分數／小數三角比、象限與正切和差」／挑戰「三角形內角、直線夾角與綜合化簡」.
- Overall:
  - The presets match the levels.
  - **Mixed numbers flood the fraction units**: 「tanα＝2(1)/(3)」, 「斜率 −3(6)/(11)」, and answers like 「1(58)/(59)（即 (117)/(59)）」. These are unprintable as textbook items.
  - `known` never gives a quadrant other than 銳角, although the tip says 「依象限補出」 and the advanced level promises 象限.
  - `identity` is pure pattern recognition at every level.
  - Missing standard types:
    - **數值角化簡**（sin20°cos40°＋cos20°sin40°）
    - **(1＋tan15°)/(1−tan15°)**
    - **tanα、tanβ 為二次方程兩根**
    - **(1＋tanα)(1＋tanβ)＝2 when α＋β＝45°**
    - **由直線方程式求夾角度數**

### Unit special 特殊角求值
- Good (large and negative angles at higher levels). Good; no change.

### Unit identity 公式展開與合併
- Variety: 1 form (「sin6xcos2y−cos6xsin2y」); challenge is identical to basic.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Advanced: numeric angles 「求 sin20°cos40°＋cos20°sin40° 的值。」 ((√3)/(2)); 「cos75°cos15°−sin75°sin15°」 (0); 「（tan20°＋tan25°）／（1−tan20°tan25°）」 (1). Challenge: 「（1＋tan15°）／（1−tan15°）」 (√3) and 「sin（x＋30°）＋sin（x−30°）化簡」 (√3 sinx).

### Unit known 已知三角比求和差角
- Plausibility: every item says 「α、β為銳角」. There are mixed-number tangents (「tanα＝2(1)/(3)、tanβ＝3(1)/(2)」), and complementary pairs that give 「sin（α＋β）＝1」.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] No mixed numbers (see X6). Advanced/challenge with quadrants: 「(π)/(2)＜α＜π，sinα＝(3)/(5)；π＜β＜(3π)/(2)，cosβ＝−(5)/(13)，求 cos（α−β）。」 ((−4/5)(−5/13)＋(3/5)(−12/13)＝(20−36)/65＝−(16)/(65)).
  2. [P2] Reject complementary pairs (sinα＝cosβ).

### Unit tangent 正切和差公式
- Variety: 1 form (two tangents → tan(α±β)).
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Challenge: 「tanα、tanβ 為 x²−5x＋6＝0 的兩根，求 tan（α＋β）。」 (5/(1−6)＝−1). Advanced: reverse 「tan（α＋β）＝3，tanα＝2，求 tanβ」 (1/7). Challenge: 「α＋β＝45°，求（1＋tanα）（1＋tanβ）」 (2).
  2. [P1] No mixed numbers.

### Unit triangle 三角形內角關係
- Plausibility: frequent degenerate right-angle cases (「sinA＝(3)/(5)、cosB＝(3)/(5)，求 sinC」 → 1, 「cosC」 → 0), plus mixed numbers.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Reject A＋B＝90°. Challenge: 「△ABC 中 tanA＝1、tanB＝2，求 tanC 及 tanA＋tanB＋tanC 與 tanA·tanB·tanC」 (tanC＝3; both 6).

### Unit lines 兩直線的夾角
- Plausibility: slopes such as 「−3(2)/(3)、2(1)/(2)」 → 「(37)/(49)」. No textbook gives slopes as mixed numbers, and nothing asks for the angle itself.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Give the lines as equations with small integer coefficients and make about half of the answers special angles: 「L₁：2x−y＋1＝0，L₂：x−3y＋2＝0，求兩直線的銳夾角。」 (m＝2, 1/3 → tanθ＝(5/3)/(5/3)＝1 → 45°). Keep tanθ answers only for non-special cases.
