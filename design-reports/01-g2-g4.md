# g2 / g3 / g4 drills — design review (國小二、三、四年級)

Reviewed state: branch `fix/math-answer-errors`, files `/home/claude/yenyu-bot/bhcs-web/tools/math/g2-drills.html`, `g3-drills.html`, `g4-drills.html`, against the sampled chunk `01-g2-g4.txt`. Correctness is not re-audited here; all findings below are about variety, difficulty ladder, plausibility/fit, wording and 108課綱 alignment. Every claim about "what the generator can/cannot produce" was checked in the source (kind lists, ranges, level gating), not inferred from the 8 samples alone.

## Summary

- Overall the three pages are solid "計算練習" generators: answers are consistent, tips are correct, print wording is natural. The main weaknesses are (a) **ladder collapse** and (b) **missing textbook question types**, not wrong content.
- **Identical advanced/challenge generators** (same kind list and same number ranges, so 進階 and 挑戰 print the same worksheet): g2 `numbers.place`, `numbers.oddeven`, `length.ruler`, `length.calc`, `length.compare`, `shapes.edgesum`, `time.units`; g4 `angle.calc`, `angle.clock`, `angle.rightangle`. g2 `numbers.compare` is *inverted* (challenge allows a bigger gap between the two numbers, which is easier).
- **Curriculum overreach at g2 basic (labelled 一年級)**: `time.units` asks 5 天＝幾小時 / 10 小時＝幾分 / 176 分＝幾小時幾分 (日↔時 and 分÷60 are 三年級); `shapes.sides` basic asks 4 個六邊形和 2 個五邊形共幾個角 (needs 6×4); `shapes.identify` asks 「正方形是不是長方形的一種？→是」 which 低年級 textbooks do not teach (and most 二年級 tests mark 「不是」).
- **Plausibility bugs teachers will notice**: g4 `addsub.word` (學校有男生 45,896 人、女生 1,402 人; 腳踏車 92 元), g4 `muldiv.word` (遊覽車最多坐 5 人), g2 `money.count` (有 3 個 10 元，全部換成 10 元硬幣，可以換幾個？), g2 `money.enough` (有 6 元想買 72 元的玩具; 想買 3 元的玩具), g2 `sub.mix` degenerate 10 − 7 ＋ 7, 5 ＋ 7 − 7, g4 `bignum.compose` 「85 個億、1 個千萬和 957 個萬」 (non-standard decomposition that invites 85|1|957).
- **Missing standard types that matter most**: g2 反求應用題 (原有多少／送出多少) and 「用 □ 列式」 from a story (the heart of 二年級 「用 □ 表示未知數」); 數字卡排最大/最小的數 (二下 & 四年級); 看月曆 figure; g4 商中間有 0 的除法; 銳角/直角/鈍角 判斷; 大數的讀法（零）; 分數/小數 word problems and 數線; 單位選擇判斷題.
- Challenge level is frequently "same forms, bigger numbers". The P1/P2 items below give each weak challenge level 1–2 genuinely harder forms taken from 參考書 標準題型.
- Wording/notation is consistent within each page; the few inconsistencies are listed as P3.

# g2-drills.html design review

## Topic: numbers — 1000 以內的數
- Levels promised: 基礎 100 以內（一年級）；進階 1000 以內、2/5/10 個一數（二上）；挑戰 1000 以內、100 個一數、倒著數（二下）.
- Overall: content is correct but thin. `place` and `oddeven` have no advanced→challenge step; `compare` ladder is inverted; the single most standard 二年級 題型 (數字卡排數) is absent.

### Unit order 數數與數的順序
- Variety: basic 3 kinds (後一個數／前一個數／中間的數), advanced 4 (後/跳數/中間/倒著跳數), challenge same 4 plus step 100. Skip-count sequences are generated from `start=step*rand(1,8|9)`, so 2-個-數 sequences never exceed ~26 and 5-個-數 never exceed ~65 — these are 一年級 numbers even at challenge (sample: 「2 個 2 個數，填入空格：12、14、16、18、（ ）」 at advanced). Missing: 數線填空, 「從 □ 往後數 3 個」, 跨百/跨千 counting, 數字卡排數.
- Difficulty: weak ladder. challenge still draws 「68 和 70 中間的數」 (n is `rand(1,top−3)` at every level).
- Plausibility/tip: fine. Tip matches.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Start skip-count sequences inside the level's range: advanced `start=rand(10,900)` rounded to the step; challenge force crossing a hundred (e.g. 「5 個 5 個數：485、490、495、（ ）、505」, 「倒著 2 個 2 個數：604、602、（ ）、598」). Keep step 100 but start at any hundred.
  2. [P1] Add kind `cards` at advanced/challenge: 「用數字卡 3、0、7 各一張，排出最大的三位數和最小的三位數。」→「730；307」 (challenge: four cards 「排出最接近 500 的數」 optional).
  3. [P2] Challenge-only kinds: 跨百 「399 的後面一個數」「500 的前面一個數」「999 和 1000 中間有沒有數？」; 「比 460 多 10 的數」「比 460 少 100 的數」 (位值跳數).
  4. [P3] Basic: add 「從 7 開始往後數 3 個是幾？」 so basic has more than 3 near-identical forms.

### Unit place 位值：百、十、個
- Variety: 3 kinds (拆／合／某位數字代表多少) at every level. advanced and challenge are the **same generator** (`n=L===0?rand(11,99):rand(101,999)`, same kind list).
- Difficulty: advanced ＝ challenge. Zero digits produce awkward wording: 「2 個百、2 個十和 0 個一合起來是多少？」, 「5 個百、0 個十和 3 個一」.
- Plausibility/tip: fine.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Make challenge different: 「13 個十是多少？」「240 是幾個十？」「1000 是幾個百？」(二下 標準), 位值展開式 「345 ＝ 300 ＋ （ ）＋ 5」, and 「十位和個位數字交換後的數」.
  2. [P1] Drop zero parts from 合/拆 wording: 「4 個百和 7 個一合起來是多少？」→407; 「407 是幾個百和幾個一？」. (Keep 0 as a *trap* only in challenge: 「3 個百和 5 個十合起來是多少？」→350.)
  3. [P2] Add 數字卡 kind here or in `order` (not both).

### Unit compare 比大小
- Variety: 3 kinds (○填＞＜／哪個大小／三數由小排到大). No 由大到小, no 4 numbers, no 「□ 可以填哪些數字」.
- Difficulty: **inverted** — gap is ≤9 at basic, ≤30 advanced, ≤99 challenge; bigger gap = easier. Challenge sample 「480 和 400 哪一個比較小？」.
- Plausibility/tip: fine.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Challenge: force same hundreds digit and swapped/close lower digits (503 vs 530, 398 vs 401, 1000 vs 999) and mixed digit counts (99 vs 100). Advanced: gap ≤ 30 inside the same hundred.
  2. [P2] Add 「由大排到小」 and 4-number ordering at challenge; add 「□ 裡可以填哪些數字：4□6 ＜ 436」→「0、1、2」 (二下 標準挑戰題).
  3. [P3] Add 「比 500 大、比 510 小的數有幾個」 at challenge.

### Unit oddeven 單數與雙數
- Variety: basic which/count; advanced & challenge which/count/list — **identical lists and ranges** (`count` a≤80, `list` a 10–90 at both).
- Difficulty: advanced ＝ challenge. `list` (write 5 odds in a 10-span) is easier than `count` over 20 numbers.
- Plausibility/tip: fine. Note: 單雙數 is a 二年級 unit in 康軒/南一/翰林, so the level text's 「基礎（一年級）」 label is off; harmless.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Challenge-only kinds: 判斷題 「圈出單數：32、35、38、41、47」; 「用 3、6、7 排出的三位數中，哪些是雙數？」; 「比 36 大的最小單數是？」.
  2. [P3] Count-range wording 「（包含 8 和 15）」 is heavy for 一年級; basic could instead give the list 「8、9、10、11、12 中有幾個雙數？」.

## Topic: add — 加法（低年級）
- Levels promised: 基礎 20 以內、湊十；進階 二位數、個位進位；挑戰 三位數、連續進位.
- Overall: solid; `add` and `carry` overlap heavily (both produce plain a＋b), and challenge `carry` is over-weighted with fill-in-the-blank which is a subtraction skill.

### Unit add 加法計算
- Variety: 3 kinds (算式／合起來／比 a 多 b). The `pair()` at advanced/challenge may or may not carry, so this unit duplicates `carry`'s `op` kind. No 幾十加幾十／幾百加幾百 (心算 標準), no 交換律 填空.
- Difficulty: ok by size.
- Plausibility/tip: tip mentions 進位 while unit name implies plain addition; fine.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Restrict `add` to non-carry pairs and add kind `tens`: 「30 ＋ 40」「300 ＋ 500」「250 ＋ 30」; leave every carrying case to `carry`. (Removes the duplicate between units.)
  2. [P3] Add 「7 ＋ 8 ＝ 8 ＋ （ ）」 交換律 at basic.

### Unit carry 進位加法
- Variety: basic op/湊十拆解/（ ）＋b＝s; advanced op/直式/a＋（ ）＝s; challenge same as advanced with 3-digit. 5 of 8 challenge samples are 「346 ＋ （ ）＝ 360」 — the "ten" kind at L≥1 is a fill-in, not 湊十, and it dominates.
- Difficulty: level text promises 連續進位 at challenge, but the filter only requires *a* carry (ones or tens).
- Plausibility/tip: tip good.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Challenge: require both carries (ones and tens) for `op`/`vert` (e.g. 476 ＋ 385), and cap fill-in to ≤1/4 of draws.
  2. [P2] Add 直式填空 kind (missing digit in a vertical sum) at advanced/challenge: 「直式中的 □ 是多少？ 4□ ＋ 27 ＝ 75」→8. (Plain text form is enough: 「4□ ＋ 27 ＝ 75，□ 是多少？」.)
  3. [P3] Rename internal kind "ten" at L≥1 to "fill" so tip/kind match.

### Unit multi 連加
- Variety: three/湊十/應用 at all levels. Challenge samples 「1 ＋ 89 ＋ 9」「8 ＋ 205 ＋ 2」 are the easy 湊十 trick; the only hard part at challenge is the word problem.
- Difficulty: ok-ish; contexts repetitive (always 媽媽給、爸爸又給).
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Challenge: three 2–3-digit numbers with at least one carry and no 湊十 pair (e.g. 148 ＋ 236 ＋ 95); keep 湊十 at basic/advanced.
  2. [P3] Vary contexts (三種顏色的珠子、三天跳繩次數、三個班級人數).

## Topic: sub — 減法（低年級）
- Levels promised: 基礎 20 以內、拆十；進階 二位數、個位退位；挑戰 三位數、十位是 0 的連續退位.
- Overall: `borrow` is the best-designed unit on the page (拆十 at basic, 連續退位 at challenge). `sub` overlaps with `borrow`; `mix` prints degenerate expressions.

### Unit sub 減法計算
- Variety: op/比 a 少 b/相差 at all levels. At advanced `b=rand(1,a−1)` so borrowing cases appear here too (sample 「44 和 29 相差多少？」), duplicating `borrow`.
- Difficulty: ok by size.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Restrict to non-borrow pairs and add 「70 − 30」「500 − 200」「460 − 60」 kind (幾十減幾十／幾百減幾百).
  2. [P2] Add explicit 驗算 kind at challenge (port g4's form): 「583 − 247（並用加法驗算）」→「336；驗算：336 ＋ 247 ＝ 583」. Tip already tells students to do this; no question asks for it.

### Unit borrow 退位減法
- Variety: basic op/拆十/( ); advanced op/直式/( ); challenge op/直式/十位是 0. Good.
- Difficulty: good ladder. Challenge `op`/`vert` only guarantee a ones-borrow; 連續退位 without a zero (e.g. 523 − 278) is not guaranteed.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Add kind `double` at challenge: both ones and tens borrow, tens ≠ 0 (「523 − 278」).
  2. [P3] Add 直式填空 「5□ − 28 ＝ 25，□ 是多少？」 at advanced.

### Unit mix 加減混合
- Variety: pm/mm/mp at all levels. b and c are drawn independently, so 「10 − 7 ＋ 7」「5 ＋ 7 − 7」「10 − 10 ＋ 9」 appear (3 of 8 basic samples). 
- Difficulty: fine by size.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Filter `b!==c` and `a!==b` (and avoid `a−b===0` as an intermediate at basic).
  2. [P3] At challenge allow four terms (a ＋ b − c − d) ≤ 999.

## Topic: word — 加減應用與 □
- Levels promised: 基礎 20 以內；進階 100 以內與 □；挑戰 1000 以內、兩步驟.
- Overall: all four units are "direct" problems; the defining 二年級 skills — 反求 (原有／送出多少) and 「把應用題列成含 □ 的算式」 — are missing. Challenge is only bigger numbers. Contexts: 6 names × 6 objects + 「盒子裡紅色／藍色」 for every unit; monotone when a sheet has 15 of them.

### Unit story 加減應用題
- Variety: join/take/total/left (4 one-step kinds). Answers give only the number (「3 張」); textbooks expect 「算式：7 − 4 ＝ 3，答：3 張」.
- Difficulty: challenge ＝ 3-digit versions of the same 4 kinds.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Add 反求 kinds at advanced/challenge: 「小明有一些貼紙，送給同學 14 張後還剩 37 張，小明原來有幾張？」(原有); 「公車上原有 32 人，到站後下車一些人，車上剩 18 人，下車幾人？」(減數); 「盒子裡原有 25 顆糖，媽媽又放進一些，現在有 41 顆，媽媽放進幾顆？」(加數).
  2. [P1] Challenge: 兩問 or 比較 included (「紅色 182 顆、藍色 51 顆，一共幾顆？紅色比藍色多幾顆？」).
  3. [P2] Put the 算式 in the answer for the teacher version: 「7 − 4 ＝ 3，答：3 張」 (same format `multiply.groups` already uses).
  4. [P3] Expand context pool: 公車上下車、圖書館借書、停車場、跳繩次數、頁數、存錢.

### Unit comparemore 比多比少
- Variety: 誰比誰多幾／比 a 多 b／比 a 少 b／「A 比 B 多 b，B 有幾」(反向). Good coverage. Missing 「少幾」 wording in the diff kind (always 「多幾」) and the two-step 「兩人合起來」.
- Difficulty: challenge ＝ 3-digit same kinds.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Challenge: 「哥哥有 24 顆彈珠，弟弟比哥哥少 9 顆，兩人一共有幾顆？」 (two-step); and 「B 比 A 少 b，A 有幾」 (reverse of the "less" kind).
  2. [P3] Randomize 「多幾」/「少幾」 in the diff kind.

### Unit box 用 □ 表示未知數
- Variety: basic 3 forms, advanced/challenge 4 forms (□＋a, a＋□, a−□, □−a). Pure equations only.
- Difficulty: ok by size.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Add kind `storyBox` (advanced/challenge): 「公車上原有一些人，到站下車 8 人後，車上還有 15 人。用 □ 列出算式，並算出原來有幾人。」→「□ − 8 ＝ 15，□ ＝ 23，答：23 人」. This is the 課綱 skill (N-2-? 「用 □ 表示未知數並列式」); the equation drills alone don't exercise it.
  2. [P3] Keep the 「□＝」 lead consistent (already fine).

### Unit twostep 兩步驟的應用題
- Variety: mm/pm/mp; same contexts as `story`. Tip says 「也可以列成一個算式」 but the answer never shows it.
- Difficulty: challenge is bigger numbers only.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Answer with 併式 in teacher version: 「14 − 3 ＋ 4 ＝ 15，答：15 張」.
  2. [P2] Challenge: include a 比多比少 step (「小美有 36 張，小華比小美多 12 張，兩人共幾張？」) and 「買兩樣東西找錢」 (「買 35 元和 48 元的東西，付 100 元，找回幾元？」).

## Topic: multiply — 乘法的意義
- Levels promised: 基礎 2、5、10；進階 2、3、4、5、8、10；挑戰 2～9、幾倍反推.
- Overall: ladder is by multiplier set, which matches 二上/二下 textbook order; `repeat` is excellent. Gaps: no 「幾個幾」 wording, no 交換律 (commutativity, a 二下 standard), `times.compare` never yields 「＝」.

### Unit groups 幾個一數與乘法算式
- Variety: 1 kind (+乘加 at challenge). Advanced sample 「每袋有 2 個餅乾，2 袋」 (2×2).
- Difficulty: ok by multiplier.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Add kind 「3 個 5 合起來是多少？用乘法算式表示。」→「5 × 3 ＝ 15」 (幾個幾, 二上 標準), and 「□ × □ ＝ □：每盤 4 顆、3 盤」 fill the algorithm skeleton.
  2. [P2] Challenge: 乘減 「每盒 8 枝，5 盒，用掉 6 枝，還剩幾枝？」 (now only 乘加).
  3. [P3] 「每袋有 2 個餅乾」→「每袋有 2 片餅乾」; at advanced require b ≥ 3.

### Unit repeat 連加與乘法
- Variety: toMul/toAdd/value; challenge plus1/minus1 (「6 × 5 − 6 ＝ 6 × （ ）」). Good.
- Difficulty: good.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P3] Cap the addend count at 6 in 3-column print (「5 ＋ 5 ＋ 5 ＋ 5 ＋ 5 ＋ 5 ＋ 5 ＋ 5」 wraps badly); keep 7–9 only in 2-column.

### Unit times 幾的幾倍
- Variety: times/倍的應用/compare at basic–advanced; challenge times/back/compare. `compare` returns `null` when `b===c`, so 「＝」 in 「＞、＜ 或 ＝」 never happens; 6 of 8 challenge samples are compare.
- Difficulty: challenge `back` (「（ ）的 8 倍是 16」) is the only harder form; 「a 的 1 倍」 appears often.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Add commutativity to compare: 「4 × 3 ○ 3 × 4」→「＝」 and 「2 × 6 ○ 3 × 4」 (equal products) so 「＝」 is real; cap compare at 1/3.
  2. [P2] Challenge: 「哥哥的貼紙是弟弟的 4 倍，弟弟有 6 張，哥哥比弟弟多幾張？」 (倍 + 比較, 二下 挑戰).
  3. [P3] Limit b＝1 to ≤10% of draws.

### Unit dots 看圖列乘法算式
- Variety: row/total/col (+take at challenge). Figure good.
- Difficulty: fine.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Add kind `both`: 「橫著看和直著看，各寫出一個乘法算式。」→「4 × 3 ＝ 12；3 × 4 ＝ 12」 (two ways, 二下 標準).

## Topic: money — 錢幣
- Levels promised: 基礎 1、5、10、50 元，100 元以內；進階 加 100、500，1000 元以內；挑戰 加 1000 元、兩樣一起買.
- Overall: good unit set; two plausibility bugs and a range mismatch; no coin figure (一年級 數錢 is figure-based in all three editions).

### Unit count 數錢
- Variety: total/換成 10 元/還差多少到整百. `toTen` draws only from {10,50,(100)} with random counts, so 「有 3 個 10 元，全部換成 10 元硬幣，可以換幾個？」 is a real output (2 of 8 basic samples). Advanced totals exceed the promised 1000 元 (「3 張 500 元… 1663 元」).
- Difficulty: fine otherwise.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] `toTen`: require at least one 50/100 coin and target coin ≠ the only coin present; add targets 5 元 and 50 元 (「1 張 100 元可以換幾個 50 元？」).
  2. [P1] Advanced: cap total ≤ 999 (at most one 500 元).
  3. [P2] Add a coin figure kind for basic (circles labelled 50/10/5/1): 「圖中一共有幾元？」.

### Unit change 付錢與找錢
- Variety: 1 kind (+兩樣 at advanced/challenge). 8/8 challenge samples are 「付 1000 元找回」.
- Difficulty: by price size only.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Add 「付了 2 張 100 元」(multi-bill payment), 「買 2 個一樣的東西」, and 「找回的錢最少要幾個硬幣／幾張鈔票」 (links to `fewest`).
  2. [P3] Challenge guarantee `two` ≥ 1/3 of draws.

### Unit fewest 最少的錢幣
- Good; no change.

### Unit enough 錢夠不夠
- Variety: enough/還要存/兩個一樣的玩具. `pc` picks 2 random coin types with counts 1–4, so holdings can be 6 元 vs price 72 元, or price 3 元.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P1] Require `have ≥ 20` and `price ≥ 10`, and `|price − have| ≤ have/2`.
  2. [P3] Vary the item (玩具 only) and the name (小明 only).

## Topic: time — 時間與日期
- Levels promised: 基礎 整點半點、經過幾小時、星期（一年級）；進階 幾時幾分、經過幾小時幾分、月曆（二上）；挑戰 任意分鐘、跨中午、跨月份.
- Overall: `read` is good. `units` is the biggest curriculum problem on the page; `calendar` lacks the 月曆 figure that defines the 二年級 unit.

### Unit read 看鐘面讀時刻
- Variety: read/再過幾小時/半點 at basic; read/再過幾分 at advanced+. Clock SVG is clear.
- Difficulty: good ladder (0/30 → 5-min → any minute).
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Add 電子鐘 kind: 「電子鐘顯示 7:30，是幾時幾分？」 (二上 標準).
  2. [P2] Add 上午/下午 at challenge: 「下午的鐘面是這樣，是下午幾時幾分？」.
  3. [P3] 畫時針分針 (blank clock + 「畫出 3 時 30 分」) if a blank-face figure is cheap to add.

### Unit elapsed 經過的時間
- Variety: basic hours/before/end; advanced hm/end/hours; challenge noon/hm/end. `hm` and `end` are the same generator at advanced and challenge.
- Difficulty: challenge samples 「從 10 時開始做功課，做了 2 小時」 are basic-level.
- Proposed changes:
  1. [P2] Challenge: force minute borrow (「從 8 時 45 分到 10 時 20 分」) and 跨中午 with minutes (「上午 10 時 40 分到下午 1 時 10 分」); drop plain `hours`/`end` with m＝0 from challenge.
  2. [P3] Add 「幾時前」 (`before`) to advanced with minutes.

### Unit calendar 星期與月曆
